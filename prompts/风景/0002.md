## 中文

**Fall Blooms**

生机勃勃的秋日清晨花朵,一簇深红和金黄色的菊花依偎在柔和的橙色万寿菊中,花瓣上闪烁着晶莹的露珠。柔和的清晨金色阳光从侧面照射,投射出温暖的色温和细腻的阴影。微距镜头捕捉天鹅绒般的花瓣质感和露珠的棱镜折射,背景呈现出奶油般的散景效果。空气中弥漫着宁静安详的氛围,淡淡的薄雾环绕着花朵,营造出空灵梦幻的清晨花园场景。

## English

**Fall Blooms**

Vibrant morning fall flowers, a cluster of crimson and golden chrysanthemums nestled among soft orange marigolds, dewdrops glistening on their petals. Soft golden morning light streams from the side, casting warm color temperature and delicate shadows across the blooms. Macro lens captures velvety petal textures and prismatic refractions in each dewdrop, with a creamy bokeh background. The atmosphere is serene and peaceful, with gentle mist swirling around the flowers, creating an ethereal and dreamlike early morning garden scene.
