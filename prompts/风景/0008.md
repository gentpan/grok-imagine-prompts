## 中文

**Forest Silk Stroll**

森林漫步,身穿飘逸的丝质长袍,轻柔的微风吹起织物,柔和的斑驳阳光,感性的摇摆,电影级特写,温暖的大地色调。透过树冠的阳光形成梦幻般的光束,营造出3000K的温暖色温。慢动作镜头捕捉丝绸的流动质感,每一道褶皱都闪烁着微光。浅景深突出丝绸织物的细腻纹理,背景中古老的橡树和蕨类植物柔和虚化。空气中弥漫着静谧沉思的氛围,飘浮的尘埃粒子在光束中舞动,营造出空灵超凡的森林漫步体验。

## English

**Forest Silk Stroll**

A forest walk in a flowing silk robe, gentle breeze lifting fabric, soft dappled sunlight, sensual sway, cinematic close-up, warm earth tones. Sunlight filtering through the canopy creates dreamy light beams with a warm 3000K color temperature. Slow motion shot captures the flowing texture of silk, every fold shimmering with subtle luminescence. Shallow depth of field highlights the delicate weave of silk fabric, while ancient oaks and ferns blur softly in the background. The atmosphere is tranquil and contemplative, with floating dust particles dancing in the light beams, creating an ethereal and transcendent forest walking experience.
