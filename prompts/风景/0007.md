## 中文

**Red Frame Ride**

一辆复古自行车在阳光明媚的乡村飞速驶下山坡,带有闪亮的红色车架和老式皮革座椅。动态追踪镜头从侧面跟随,捕捉运动模糊和速度感。明亮的午后阳光照射,高对比度使红色车架在金色麦田和翠绿山丘的背景下熠熠生辉。铬合金把手和辐条反射着耀眼的阳光,磨损的棕色皮革座椅展现出复古质感。清新的乡村空气充满活力和自由的氛围,浅景深使背景呈现柔美的散景效果。

## English

**Red Frame Ride**

A vintage bicycle speeding down a hill in a sunny countryside, with a shiny red frame and an old-school leather seat. Dynamic tracking shot from the side captures motion blur and sense of speed. Bright afternoon sunlight with high contrast makes the red frame gleam against golden wheat fields and emerald green hills. Chrome handlebars and spokes reflect dazzling sunlight, while the weathered brown leather seat shows authentic vintage texture. The crisp countryside air fills with an atmosphere of vitality and freedom, shallow depth of field creates beautiful bokeh in the background.
