## 中文

**山坡速降**

一辆经典的1970年代复古自行车正在翠绿的山坡上飞速驶下，速度感十足。镜头采用跟随拍摄手法，从侧面捕捉自行车的运动轨迹，背景因速度产生轻微的运动模糊效果。清晨的柔和阳光从山脊后方照射过来，形成美丽的逆光效果，自行车轮辐在光线下闪烁。山坡上野花点缀，微风吹过草丛形成波浪般的动态。远景是连绵起伏的山峦，天空湛蓝，几缕白云飘过。摄像机角度略微倾斜，增强动感和紧张感。色彩饱和度适中，呈现出清新自然的户外运动氛围。

## English

**Hill Speed**

A classic 1970s vintage bicycle speeding rapidly down a lush green hillside, conveying strong sense of motion and velocity. Camera employs tracking shot technique, capturing the bicycle's trajectory from a side angle with slight motion blur in the background due to speed. Soft morning sunlight illuminates from behind the ridge, creating beautiful backlight effect with bicycle spokes gleaming in the light. Hillside dotted with wildflowers, gentle breeze creating wave-like motion through grass. Distant rolling hills and azure sky with wispy white clouds in the background. Camera angle slightly tilted to enhance dynamic tension and excitement. Moderate color saturation presenting fresh, natural outdoor sports atmosphere with cinematic quality.
