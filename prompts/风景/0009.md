## 中文

**Dewy Grass Star**

一位年轻女子 拥有飘逸的深色头发 躺在沾满露珠的草地上 在广袤的星云天空下, 睁大好奇的眼睛 映照着星座, 脸颊上的柔和红晕, 轻柔的夜风, 以照片级真实风格.

## English

**Dewy Grass Star**

A young woman with flowing dark hair lying on dewy grass under a vast starry nebula sky, wide curious eyes reflecting constellations, soft blush on cheeks, gentle night breeze, in photoreal style.
