## 中文

**山谷中的樱花小径**

春日清晨，一条蜿蜒的石板小径穿过樱花林，花瓣随微风飘落形成粉色花雨。小径两侧是盛开的樱花树，阳光透过花枝洒下斑驳光影。远处山谷云雾缭绕，传统日式木屋若隐若现。一位身穿浅色连衣裙的少女背对镜头，漫步在小径上，长发随风轻扬。摄像机采用缓慢的 dolly forward 跟随拍摄，焦点在少女背影与飘舞的花瓣上，背景自然虚化。清晨柔和的自然光，暖色调，电影级色彩分级。花瓣在空中的动态感，营造宁静诗意的氛围。高分辨率自然风景摄影风格。

## English

**Cherry Blossom Path in the Valley**

Spring morning, a winding stone path cuts through a cherry blossom forest, petals falling with the breeze forming pink flower rain. Cherry trees in full bloom line both sides of the path, sunlight filtering through branches casting dappled light and shadow. In the distance, mist wraps around the valley with traditional Japanese wooden houses faintly visible. A young girl in a light-colored dress, back to the camera, strolls along the path, long hair swaying in the wind. Camera uses a slow dolly forward tracking shot, focus on the girl's silhouette and dancing petals, background naturally blurred. Soft natural morning light, warm tones, cinematic color grading. Dynamic sense of petals in the air, creating a tranquil poetic atmosphere. High-resolution natural landscape photography style.
