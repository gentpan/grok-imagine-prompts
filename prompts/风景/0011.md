## 中文

**I used to be able**

我过去能够将我拍摄的照片制作成宝可梦和数码宝贝动漫风格。从Shutterstock偷来的风景图像也一样。现在什么都做不了。

## English

**I used to be able**

I used to be able to get photos I took made into Pokémon and Digimon anime style. Same with stolen landscape images from Shutterstock. Now it cannot get anything at all.
