## 中文

**林间小憩的狗**

在被茂密森林环绕的温馨木质小屋内，一只金毛猎犬正惬意地蜷缩在壁炉旁的毛毯上小憩。午后的阳光透过格子窗户洒进来，在木地板上投下斑驳的光影。壁炉中的火光温暖地跳动着，为整个场景增添了橙黄色的暖调。镜头采用广角视角，从室内一角拍摄，营造出宁静祥和的氛围。窗外隐约可见绿色的森林背景，室内光线柔和，色调温暖自然。木质家具纹理清晰，营造出乡村田园风格的舒适感。

## English

**Napping Dog**

Inside a cozy wooden cottage surrounded by dense forest, a golden retriever is comfortably curled up on a blanket near the fireplace, peacefully napping. Afternoon sunlight streams through latticed windows, casting dappled shadows on the wooden floor. The fireplace's warm glow flickers gently, adding orange-amber hues to the entire scene. Wide-angle camera perspective from a corner of the room, creating a serene and tranquil atmosphere. Blurred green forest visible through windows in the background, soft interior lighting with warm natural color tones. Clear wooden furniture textures conveying rustic countryside comfort. Dust particles visible in the sunbeams, adding depth and realism to the peaceful domestic scene.
