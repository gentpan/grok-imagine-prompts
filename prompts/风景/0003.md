## 中文

**Terrace Morning**

4K超写实电影级竖版肖像（9:16）。场景：现代都市热带露台——哑光黑色木质墙面攀爬着绿植，深色石质地板，木质沙发搭配翡翠绿靠垫，圆形木桌上摆放着零食和饮品。温暖的吊灯在上方柔和地弧形悬挂。人物：一位年轻男子，休闲酷感风格，放松地坐在角落，周围环绕着茂盛的热带植物。表情：平静，正面朝向镜头。服装：合身白色T恤，灰色运动裤，白色运动鞋。姿势：向后倚靠，一腿交叉，一手搭在沙发上，另一手拿着咖啡杯。镜头：徕卡Summilux 50mm f/1.4——浅景深，主体清晰对焦，背景虚化散景。光线：柔和的晨露光线从叶片间过滤而入（右上方），薄雾般的光晕，细微视差效果，精致胶片颗粒感，梦幻色彩调级——深绿与暖橙色调。氛围：宁静反思的清晨时刻。构图：前景植物和桌边框住主体；湿润的石质地板映出温柔倒影。

## English

**Terrace Morning**

4K ultra-realistic cinematic portrait (9:16). Scene: Modern urban-tropical terrace — matte black wooden walls with climbing plants, dark stone floor, wooden sofa with emerald-green cushions, and a round wooden table with snacks and drinks. Warm hanging bulbs arc softly above. Subject: Young man with a cool casual style sitting relaxed in the corner, surrounded by lush tropical plants. Expression: Calm, facing forward. Outfit: Fitted white T-shirt, gray joggers, white sneakers. Pose: Leaned back, one leg crossed, one hand on the sofa, other holding a coffee cup. Lens: Leica Summilux 50mm f/1.4 — shallow depth of field, subject in sharp focus with bokeh background. Lighting: Soft morning dew light filtering through leaves (upper right), misty glow, subtle parallax, fine film grain, dreamy color grading — deep greens and warm oranges. Mood: Peaceful, reflective morning. Composition: Foreground plants and table edge framing the subject; wet stone floor with gentle reflections.
