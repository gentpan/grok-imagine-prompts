**Generated on Seedance 2.0 and**

Generated on Seedance 2.0 and Grok Imagine 1.5 preview API. 6-8 second clips, horror genre prompts, tested both with and without negative prompts. Create a horror film-style test comparison scene: use dark low-key lighting (2700-3200K color temperature) emphasizing deep shadows and high contrast. Employ handheld camera micro-shake effects with slow push-in movements to build tense, uneasy atmosphere. Visuals should include typical horror elements—dimly lit corridors, eerie silhouettes, or sudden visual jolts—combined with fog and grain texture. Use wide-angle lens (16-24mm) and asymmetric composition to create disturbing visual tension and suspense.
