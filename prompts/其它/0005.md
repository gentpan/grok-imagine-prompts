## 中文

**However, his family members have**

然而，他的家人已经关闭了他的银行和储蓄账户并提取了所有资金，他们与Santander UK签署了赔偿表，显然对于余额低于5万英镑的账户，这与死亡证明和身份证件一起被认为是足够的权利证明。创建一个严肃的纪实风格场景：在冷色调灯光下（色温4000K），展示银行内部环境——大理石柜台的光滑质感、玻璃隔板的反光、文件纸张的细腻纹理。使用中景镜头（35-50mm）和水平视角，营造正式的商务氛围。添加浅景深效果，让前景的法律文件清晰锐利，背景的银行环境微微虚化。冷静的侧光照明勾勒出文件边缘和印章的立体感，创造出充满张力和正式感的视觉叙事。

## English

**However, his family members have**

However, his family members have closed his bank and savings accounts and withdrawn all funds, they signed the indemnity form with Santander UK and apparently for balances under £50k this alongside death certificate and ID is considered sufficient proof of entitlement. Create a serious documentary-style scene: under cool-toned lighting (4000K color temperature), show a bank interior—smooth marble counter textures, glass partition reflections, fine paper document details. Use mid-range lens (35-50mm) with level perspective to establish formal business atmosphere. Add shallow depth of field so foreground legal documents appear sharp while background bank environment gently blurs. Calm side lighting defines document edges and stamp dimensionality, creating a tense, formal visual narrative.
