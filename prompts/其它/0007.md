## 中文

**After all of our references**

在添加了所有参考和描述后，创建专业图像就变得如此简单："嘿，请画我在公园里穿着我平时的风格"，不需要再次提供参考或描述。

## English

**After all of our references**

After all of our references and descriptions are added creating professional images is as simple as "oh hey draw me in a park wearing my usual style please," without references or description given again.
