## 中文

**After all of our references**

在添加了所有参考和描述后，创建专业图像就变得如此简单："嘿，请画我在公园里穿着我平时的风格"，不需要再次提供参考或描述。创建一个自然轻松的公园场景：使用温暖的自然日光（色温5500K），展现午后的金色时光。采用50mm标准镜头和f/2.8光圈，营造浅景深效果——主体人物清晰锐利，背景的树木和草地柔和虚化。光线从侧面斜射，勾勒出人物轮廓和服装质感。捕捉自然的肢体语言和放松的表情，展现真实的个人风格细节——服装面料的质感、配饰的反光、发丝的细节。整体氛围应轻松愉快，充满生活气息和个性化表达。

## English

**After all of our references**

After all of our references and descriptions are added creating professional images is as simple as "oh hey draw me in a park wearing my usual style please," without references or description given again. Create a natural, relaxed park scene: use warm natural daylight (5500K color temperature) capturing that golden afternoon hour. Employ 50mm standard lens with f/2.8 aperture for shallow depth of field—subject in sharp focus while background trees and grass gently blur. Light comes from the side at an angle, defining subject silhouette and clothing textures. Capture natural body language and relaxed expressions, revealing authentic personal style details—fabric textures, accessory reflections, hair strand details. Overall atmosphere should feel casual and joyful, full of lifestyle authenticity and personalized expression.
