## 中文

**Prompt 1**

[视频-文本转视频 — Grok Imagine 1.5] 一部电影级爱情剧,15秒,带有英语对白,口型同步。多个摄像机角度,镜头间硬切。主题:一位22岁的美国大学男生,穿着休闲毛衣和牛仔裤,在柔和的午后光线中站在砖砌大学建筑后面。他把一小束花藏在背后。一位21岁的美国女生,穿着轻便夹克,单肩背包,面对着他。按时间顺序的镜头和对白:0-3秒:广角镜头,两人可见。男生移动重心,艰难地吞咽,从背后拿出花束并伸出去。他说话,声音颤抖:"我...我想告诉你这个已经很久了。我喜欢你。"3-6秒:硬切到女生脸部的紧密特写。她的表情带着遗憾变柔和,眼睛低垂。她轻声回答:"对不起。我不能。我要离开这个国家。"6-9秒:硬切到男生脸部的紧密特写,锁定且静止。他充满希望的表情凝固,笑容消失,眼睛泛起泪光。他保持沉默。9-12秒:硬切到从男生背后的广角镜头。女生转身走进背景,变得越来越小。他静止不动,花束垂到身侧。12-15秒:硬切到男生脸部的极端特写,静态取景。一滴眼泪涌上,下巴颤抖,他用力眨眼并强挤出颤抖的半笑。他低声说,声音哽咽:"我没事。"摄像机:每个镜头都是锁定的静态画面——没有缩放或漂移。过渡仅为干净的硬切。浅景深,温暖的金色逆光,柔和的校园散景。声音:柔和的校园午后环境音,微弱的鸟鸣,轻柔的风声。低沉忧郁的钢琴在6秒时进入并逐渐增强,在最后几秒达到顶峰。清晰的英语对白——男生的声音紧张然后哽咽,女生的声音柔和而带有歉意。风格:电影级,胶片颗粒,温暖的去饱和色调,情感独立剧风格,最后画面停留。

[VIDEO-T2V — Grok Imagine 1.5] A cinematic romance drama, 15 seconds, with spoken English dialogue, lip-synced. Multiple camera angles with hard cuts between shots. Subject: A 22-year-old American college man, casual sweater and jeans, behind a brick university building in soft late-afternoon light. He hides a small bouquet of flowers behind his back. A 21-year-old American woman, light jacket, backpack on one shoulder, faces him. Shots and dialogue in timeline order: 0-3s: WIDE SHOT, both visible. The man shifts his weight, swallows hard, pulls the bouquet from behind his back and holds it out. He speaks, voice shaky: "I... I've wanted to tell you this for so long. I like you." 3-6s: HARD CUT to a TIGHT CLOSE-UP on the woman's face. Her expression softens with regret, eyes lowering. She replies softly: "I'm sorry. I can't. I'm leaving the country." 6-9s: HARD CUT to a TIGHT CLOSE-UP on the man's face, locked and static. His hopeful look freezes, his smile fades, his eyes glisten. He stays silent. 9-12s: HARD CUT to a WIDE SHOT from behind the man. The woman turns and walks away into the background, growing smaller. He stands still, the bouquet lowering to his side. 12-15s: HARD CUT to an EXTREME CLOSE-UP on the man's face, static framing. A single tear brims, his jaw trembles, he blinks hard and forces a shaky half-smile. He whispers, voice breaking: "I'm okay." Camera: Each shot is a locked, static frame — no zooming or drifting. Transitions are clean hard cuts only. Shallow depth of field, warm golden backlight, soft campus bokeh. Sound: Soft ambient campus afternoon, faint birds, gentle wind. A low melancholic piano enters at 6s and swells, peaking in the final seconds. Clear spoken English dialogue — the man's voice nervous then breaking, the woman's soft and apologetic.
