**Seedance 2.0 produces noticeably stronger**

Seedance 2.0 produces noticeably stronger audio. Audio mix sounded mastered, not generated. Wind, ambient, and on-subject sound sat at the right levels. But it ignored the compound camera direction across all 4 tests.
