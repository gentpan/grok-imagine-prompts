## 中文

**Amazon's shopping app will now**

亚马逊购物应用现在会在客户在搜索栏中输入文字时创建实际上不存在的产品的AI生成图像，目的是帮助购物者找到类似这些虚假图片的真实商品。创建一个现代科技感的界面场景：采用明亮的屏幕光照（色温6500K），展示智能手机界面的玻璃质感和高光反射。使用浅景深（f/2.8）聚焦于手机屏幕，背景柔和虚化。屏幕上显示AI生成的产品图像——逼真的材质纹理、精致的产品细节、动态的加载动画。采用45度俯视角度和柔和的环境光，营造出互动式的数字购物体验。添加微妙的UI元素光晕效果和平滑的渐变过渡，传达出智能化和未来感的视觉氛围。

## English

**Amazon's shopping app will now**

Amazon's shopping app will now create AI-generated images of products that don't actually exist as customers type words into the search bar, with the goal of helping shoppers find real items that resemble those fake pictures. Create a modern tech-savvy interface scene: use bright screen illumination (6500K color temperature) showing smartphone interface glass textures and highlight reflections. Apply shallow depth of field (f/2.8) focused on phone screen with softly blurred background. Screen displays AI-generated product images—realistic material textures, refined product details, dynamic loading animations. Use 45-degree overhead angle with soft ambient lighting to create interactive digital shopping experience. Add subtle UI element glow effects and smooth gradient transitions, conveying intelligent, futuristic visual atmosphere.
