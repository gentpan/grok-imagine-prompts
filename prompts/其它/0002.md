## 中文

**图像质量技术诊断对比**

详细的技术诊断场景：在中性灰色背景（色温6500K）下，使用分屏对比展示图像质量差异。左侧显示理想的高清图像——细腻平滑的渐变、锐利的边缘细节、丰富的色彩层次；右侧展示低质量版本——明显的颗粒噪点、压缩色块、模糊的纹理细节。采用微距镜头（100mm）放大像素级差异，使用柔和均匀的顶光照明，消除阴影干扰。添加数字标注和测量标线，标示文件大小（左侧2.5MB，右侧250KB）、分辨率参数（左侧4K，右侧720p），营造专业的技术分析氛围。科学教育风格，精确的图表与数据可视化。

## English

**Image Quality Technical Diagnostic Comparison**

Detailed technical diagnostic scene: split-screen comparison on a neutral gray background (6500K color temperature) showing image quality differences. Left side displays ideal high-resolution image—smooth gradients, sharp edge details, rich color depth; right side shows low-quality version—visible grain noise, compression artifacts, blurred texture details. Use macro lens (100mm) to magnify pixel-level differences with soft, even top lighting to eliminate shadow interference. Add digital annotations and measurement guides showing file sizes (left 2.5MB, right 250KB), resolution parameters (left 4K, right 720p), creating a professional technical analysis atmosphere. Scientific educational style, precise charts and data visualization.
