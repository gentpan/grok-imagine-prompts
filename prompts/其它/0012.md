## 中文

**漂浮的时间碎片**

超现实场景：无尽的白色虚空空间中，数十个半透明的时钟与齿轮机械在空中缓慢旋转漂浮。每个时钟显示不同的时间，有的指针正向旋转，有的逆向转动。一位身穿现代休闲装的年轻男子站在虚空中，伸手触碰一个漂浮的怀表，手指穿过表面产生涟漪效果。他的身体周围环绕着淡蓝色的时间轨迹线条，延伸至无限远处。摄像机采用缓慢的环绕 dolly 运动，展现整个超现实空间的层次感。柔和的侧光照明，投射出长长的影子。时钟齿轮带有金属质感，但透明度 50%，营造梦幻氛围。概念艺术风格，电影级照明与色彩分级。

## English

**Floating Time Fragments**

Surreal scene: In an endless white void space, dozens of translucent clocks and mechanical gears slowly rotate and float in the air. Each clock displays a different time, some hands spinning forward, others backward. A young man in modern casual wear stands in the void, reaching to touch a floating pocket watch, his fingers passing through its surface creating ripple effects. Around his body, light blue temporal trajectory lines extend to infinity. Camera uses a slow circling dolly movement to reveal the layered depth of the surreal space. Soft side lighting casts long shadows. Clock gears have metallic texture but 50% transparency, creating a dreamlike atmosphere. Concept art style, cinematic lighting and color grading.
