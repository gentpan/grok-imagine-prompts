## 中文

**Subject occupies 70-85% of frame.**

主体占据画面的70-85%。手、武器、珠宝和纹理工作都以细节渲染。背景是装饰性饰品或图案,而不是环境。构图呈现为角色研究或时尚社论。

## English

**Subject occupies 70-85% of frame.**

Subject occupies 70-85% of frame. Hands, weapons, jewelry, and texture work all rendered in detail. Background is decorative ornament or pattern, not environment. Composition reads as character study or fashion editorial.
