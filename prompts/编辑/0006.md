## 中文

**Add Sports Car**

这需要一些活力——在旁边加一辆光滑的红色跑车怎么样？那会让它更上一层楼！在画面右侧或左侧三分之一处，放置一辆流线型的法拉利红色跑车（色值#FF2800），车身呈现镜面般的光泽反射，高光处闪烁着金属质感。使用轻微的广角镜头（24-35mm），以低角度拍摄强调车辆的动感线条。添加地面的微妙倒影，营造湿润路面的质感。配合戏剧性的侧光照明，突出车身曲线和空气动力学设计，创造出充满速度感和奢华气息的视觉冲击。

## English

**Add Sports Car**

This needs some pizzazz—how about sticking a slick red sports car over on the side? That'd totally kick it up a notch! Position a streamlined Ferrari-red sports car (#FF2800) in the right or left third of the frame, body gleaming with mirror-like reflections and metallic highlights. Use a slight wide-angle lens (24-35mm) at a low angle to emphasize the vehicle's dynamic lines. Add subtle ground reflections suggesting wet pavement texture. Apply dramatic side lighting to accentuate body curves and aerodynamic design, creating a visual impact full of speed, luxury, and excitement.
