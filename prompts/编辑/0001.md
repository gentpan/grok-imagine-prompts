## 中文

**Brighten Up**

嘿，这张照片太暗了——你能调亮点吗？让它感觉阳光明媚、快乐，就像完美的一天。增加温暖的金色阳光，色温调至5500-6500K范围，营造清晨或午后的柔和光线。提升整体曝光度和亮度，让阴影区域也充满细节。增强环境光的扩散效果，创造出轻松愉悦的氛围，仿佛置身于完美天气下的户外场景，空气中弥漫着温暖明亮的感觉。

## English

**Brighten Up**

Hey, this pic's way too dim—can you lighten it up? Make it feel all sunny and happy, like a perfect day. Add warm golden sunlight with color temperature around 5500-6500K, creating that soft morning or afternoon glow. Boost overall exposure and brightness so even the shadow areas reveal detail. Enhance ambient light diffusion to create a breezy, cheerful atmosphere—like you're outdoors in perfect weather with warm, luminous air all around.
