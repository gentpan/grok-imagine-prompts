## 中文

**Cute Beagle**

这张照片缺少一些可爱感——在前面加一只毛茸茸的小比格犬怎么样？每次看到都会让我心融化！在画面前景的中心偏下位置，放置一只可爱的比格犬幼犬，具有经典的三色毛发（黑、棕、白），大大的棕色眼睛充满好奇。使用浅景深（f/2.0-f/2.8）和50-85mm镜头，让小狗清晰锐利而背景柔和虚化。添加温暖的自然光从侧前方照射，突出毛发的蓬松质感和细腻纹理。小狗的姿态应该生动自然——也许是歪着头、竖起耳朵，或露出粉嫩的舌头，创造出让人无法抗拒的萌态和治愈感。

## English

**Cute Beagle**

This pic's missing some cuteness—how about popping in a scruffy little beagle right up front? I'd melt every time I see it! Position an adorable beagle puppy in the lower-center foreground, featuring classic tri-color fur (black, tan, white) and big curious brown eyes. Use shallow depth of field (f/2.0-f/2.8) with a 50-85mm lens to keep the puppy razor-sharp while the background gently blurs. Add warm natural lighting from the front-side to highlight the fluffy fur texture and delicate details. The puppy's pose should be lively and natural—maybe head tilted, ears perked, or tongue out—creating an irresistible, heartwarming charm.
