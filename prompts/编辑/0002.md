## 中文

**Bold Colors**

颜色还不错，但我想让它们真正跳出来——你能增强它们，让它们超级大胆生动吗？增加饱和度至120-140%，让原色变得更加浓郁鲜艳。提升色彩鲜明度，使红、蓝、黄等主色调呈现出强烈的视觉冲击力。调整色调映射以增强色彩深度和丰富度，创造出充满活力、引人注目的视觉效果，仿佛每种颜色都在画面中跃动，散发着生机勃勃的能量。

## English

**Bold Colors**

The colors are decent, but I want them to really jump out at me—can you boost them so they're super bold and lively? Increase saturation to 120-140%, making the primary colors rich and vibrant. Enhance color vibrancy so reds, blues, and yellows deliver strong visual impact. Adjust tone mapping to deepen color richness and create an energetic, eye-catching effect—like every hue is dancing across the frame, radiating lively energy and visual excitement.
