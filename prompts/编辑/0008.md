## 中文

**Zap Tree**

右边那棵树让我很烦——它挡住了一切。你能把它去掉吗？使用智能内容识别技术，完全移除右侧的树木及其阴影。填充被遮挡的区域时，要匹配周围环境的光照条件、色调和纹理细节——无论是天空的云层渐变、建筑物的表面质感，还是地面的材质。确保移除后的区域与原场景无缝融合，保持光线的一致性和透视关系的连贯性，创造出自然流畅、毫无痕迹的视觉效果，仿佛树木从未存在过。

## English

**Zap Tree**

That tree on the right is bugging me—it's blocking everything. Can you zap it out of there? Use intelligent content-aware removal to completely eliminate the tree and its shadows on the right side. When filling the occluded area, match the surrounding environment's lighting conditions, color tones, and texture details—whether sky gradients, building surfaces, or ground materials. Ensure the removed region blends seamlessly with the original scene, maintaining lighting consistency and perspective continuity for a natural, trace-free result, as if the tree never existed.
