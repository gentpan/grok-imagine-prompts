## 中文

**情感氛围转换**

将这个场景的情感基调从原本的平静转变为忧郁深沉的氛围。通过调整色彩饱和度降低至冷色调，增强阴影对比营造戏剧性，添加细微的雨滴或雾气特效增强情绪感染力。光线从明亮柔和转变为柔和漫射的灰蓝色调，人物表情或姿态通过微妙的动画调整传递内心的复杂情感。整体视觉风格偏向电影级叙事美学，强调情感的深度与层次，搭配缓慢的镜头推进强化沉浸感。

## English

**Emotional Atmosphere Transformation**

Transform the emotional tone of this scene from its original calmness to a melancholic and profound atmosphere. Adjust color saturation to cooler tones, enhance shadow contrast for dramatic effect, and add subtle rain droplet or mist effects to amplify emotional resonance. Lighting shifts from bright and soft to diffused gray-blue tones, with character expressions or postures conveying complex inner emotions through subtle animation adjustments. Overall visual style leans toward cinematic narrative aesthetics, emphasizing emotional depth and layers, accompanied by slow camera push-in movements to enhance immersion.
