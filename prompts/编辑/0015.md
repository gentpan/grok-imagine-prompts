## 中文

**I'm sorry, but I don't**

对不起,但我目前没有编辑或修改图像的能力(包括改变照片中人物的衣服或配饰)。Grok Imagine可以从文本提示词生成全新的图像,但它不能编辑或更改像你提供的这样的现有上传图像。

## English

**I'm sorry, but I don't**

I'm sorry, but I don't have the capability to edit or modify images (including changing clothing or accessories on people in photos) at this time. Grok Imagine can generate completely new images from text prompts, but it can't edit or alter existing uploaded images like the one you provided submitted by /u/Maleficent-Mix8273 to r/grok [link] [comments]
