## 中文

**四季风格转换**

将一张春天公园照片转换为四季不同版本。保持原始构图、建筑物与道路位置不变，仅改变季节特征：春季版本保留原样，樱花盛开，嫩绿草地，温暖阳光。夏季版本增强绿色饱和度，树木枝叶茂密，天空湛蓝，阳光强烈。秋季版本转换树叶为橙红黄色调，地面铺满落叶，柔和暖色调照明。冬季版本添加白雪覆盖地面与树枝，天空灰白，冷色调，雾气效果。每个版本保持相同的光照方向与阴影角度，确保视角与透视完全一致。写实摄影风格，自然过渡，高分辨率细节。

## English

**Seasonal Style Transformation**

Transform a spring park photo into four seasonal versions. Keep the original composition, building and road positions unchanged, only alter seasonal characteristics: Spring version remains as is, cherry blossoms in bloom, tender green grass, warm sunlight. Summer version enhances green saturation, trees with dense foliage, bright blue sky, intense sunlight. Autumn version converts leaves to orange-red-yellow tones, ground covered with fallen leaves, soft warm-tone lighting. Winter version adds white snow covering ground and branches, gray-white sky, cold tones, fog effect. Each version maintains the same lighting direction and shadow angles, ensuring perspective and viewpoint are completely consistent. Photorealistic photography style, natural transitions, high-resolution details.
