## 中文

**So you can imagine my**

所以你可以想象我的困惑，当我看到一个著名的素食主义活动家说某人仍然是素食主义者，同时却与一匹马保持不公平的关系，为了自己的私利利用它们。创建一个概念性的对比场景：一侧是站在讲台上的活动家形象，沐浴在明亮的舞台灯光下（色温5000K），背景是支持的人群；另一侧是昏暗环境（色温3000K）中一匹马被束缚的画面，采用低角度镜头和冷色调滤镜。使用分屏构图或渐变过渡，营造出视觉上的道德张力。画面应具有纪实摄影的质感和深度，传达出伦理困境的复杂性。

## English

**So you can imagine my**

So you can imagine my confusion when Ive see a large prominent vegan activist saying someone's still vegan while engaging in an unfair relationship with a horse, using them for their own personal gain. Create a conceptual contrasting scene: one side showing an activist figure on a podium bathed in bright stage lighting (5000K color temperature) with a supportive crowd backdrop; the other side depicting a horse in dim conditions (3000K) in a constrained environment, using low-angle camera work and cool-toned filters. Employ split-screen composition or gradient transition to create visual moral tension. The image should have documentary photography texture and depth, conveying the complexity of ethical dilemmas.
