## 中文

**Good Vibes Text**

我想让它表达些什么——能否在顶部添加大而厚重的白色字母，写上"Good Vibes Only"？这会营造出氛围。使用粗体无衬线字体（如Montserrat Bold或Futura Heavy），字号约为画面宽度的1/8。文字应采用纯白色（#FFFFFF），带有柔和的投影效果（透明度30-40%，偏移3-5像素），增强可读性和立体感。可选添加微妙的外发光效果或描边，确保文字在任何背景上都清晰醒目。文字排列居中对齐，位于画面上方1/6处，创造出积极向上、充满正能量的视觉宣言。

## English

**Good Vibes Text**

I want this to say something—can you add big, chunky white letters at the top that say 'Good Vibes Only'? That'd set the mood. Use bold sans-serif typography (like Montserrat Bold or Futura Heavy), sized to roughly 1/8 of the frame width. Text should be pure white (#FFFFFF) with soft drop shadow (30-40% opacity, 3-5px offset) for enhanced readability and dimension. Optionally add subtle outer glow or stroke to ensure clarity against any background. Center-align the text in the upper sixth of the frame, creating an uplifting, positive-energy visual statement.
