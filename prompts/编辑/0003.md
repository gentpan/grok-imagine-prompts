## 中文

**Pop Contrast**

这里的一切都有点模糊——你能调整对比度吗？我想让细节突出，这样我才能真正看清发生了什么。增强明暗对比度至高动态范围，让高光区域更明亮，阴影部分更深邃。锐化边缘细节，提升纹理清晰度和微观层次，使材质表面的质感——无论是织物纹理、金属光泽还是皮肤细节——都变得清晰可辨。调整中间调分离度，创造出层次分明、细节丰富的视觉效果，每个元素都具有明确的轮廓和深度。

## English

**Pop Contrast**

Everything's kinda mushy here—can you tweak the contrast? I want the details to pop so I can actually see what's happening. Enhance the contrast to high dynamic range, making highlights brighter and shadows deeper. Sharpen edge definition and boost texture clarity so surface details—whether fabric weaves, metallic sheens, or skin textures—become crisp and distinct. Adjust midtone separation to create layered, detail-rich visuals where every element has clear definition and depth.
