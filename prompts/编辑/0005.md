**Center Crop**

边缘周围有很多额外的杂物——你能把它裁剪到只剩中心的那个人吗？反正他才是主角。

## English

**Center Crop**

There’s all this extra junk around the edges—can you chop it down to just the guy in the center? He’s the star of the show anyway.
