## 中文

**Center Crop**

边缘周围有很多额外的杂物——你能把它裁剪到只剩中心的那个人吗？反正他才是主角。使用紧凑的框架构图，采用中心对称布局，将主体人物置于画面正中并占据2/3画幅。应用浅景深效果（f/2.8-f/4），让背景自然虚化，突出主体的清晰度和细节。调整焦点，使人物的面部特征、服装质感和身体轮廓都获得最佳呈现，创造出肖像级的视觉聚焦效果，消除干扰元素，让观众的注意力完全集中在核心主角身上。

## English

**Center Crop**

There's all this extra junk around the edges—can you chop it down to just the guy in the center? He's the star of the show anyway. Use tight framing with center-aligned composition, positioning the subject dead-center and filling 2/3 of the frame. Apply shallow depth of field (f/2.8-f/4) to naturally blur the background, emphasizing the subject's sharpness and detail. Adjust focus so facial features, clothing textures, and body contours are optimally rendered, creating portrait-level visual focus that eliminates distractions and draws all attention to the main character.
