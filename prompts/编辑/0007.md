## 中文

**Soaring Birds**

天空看起来有些空旷静止，能否加入一些翱翔的飞鸟？这会让画面感觉更有活力和自由感。在天空的中上部区域，添加5-8只鸟类剪影，以V字形或散开的自然编队飞行。使用动态模糊效果表现飞行中的翼展动作，营造运动感。调整鸟群的大小透视，远处的较小，近处的稍大，增强空间深度。配合柔和的逆光或侧光，让鸟儿的轮廓在天空背景中清晰可辨，创造出充满生机、自由翱翔的诗意氛围，为静态天空注入流动的生命力。

## English

**Soaring Birds**

The sky's just sitting there—can you throw in some birds soaring around? It'd make it feel more alive and free. Add 5-8 bird silhouettes in the upper-mid sky region, flying in V-formation or naturally scattered patterns. Use motion blur to convey wingbeat dynamics and movement. Adjust bird sizing with perspective—smaller in distance, slightly larger up close—to enhance spatial depth. Apply gentle backlighting or side-lighting so their silhouettes stand out against the sky, creating a poetic atmosphere of vitality and freedom, infusing the static sky with flowing life energy.
