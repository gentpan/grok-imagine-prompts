## 中文

**赛博朋克风格转换**

将一张普通街景照片转换为赛博朋克风格。保留原始构图与主体，但增强霓虹色调：粉红色、青色、紫色光晕。添加数字全息投影广告牌、悬浮的无人机、雨后湿滑的反光路面。强化对比度，暗部更深，高光区域泛出炫目的霓虹光芒。在建筑物表面添加日文与英文混合的发光招牌。增加电影级色彩分级，Blade Runner 风格的雾气效果，镜头光晕与光线散射。保持原照片中人物或车辆的位置不变，仅改变周围环境氛围与色彩。

## English

**Cyberpunk Style Transformation**

Transform an ordinary street scene photo into cyberpunk style. Preserve the original composition and subjects, but enhance neon tones: pink, cyan, purple halos. Add digital holographic billboards, hovering drones, rain-slicked reflective pavement. Intensify contrast, deeper shadows, highlight areas glowing with dazzling neon light. Add luminous signs mixing Japanese and English on building surfaces. Apply cinematic color grading, Blade Runner-style fog effects, lens flares and light scattering. Keep the position of people or vehicles in the original photo unchanged, only alter the surrounding environmental atmosphere and colors.
