## 中文

**工业厂房中的工人肖像**

一位 40 多岁的男性工人站在大型工业厂房内，背景是巨大的金属机械与传送带系统。他穿着深蓝色工装，双手插在口袋里，脸上带着疲惫但坚毅的表情。厂房内的荧光灯从上方投射冷白色光线，与侧面透过高窗的自然暖光形成对比，在他脸部创造出 split lighting 效果。采用 35mm f/2 镜头，中景构图，焦点在工人面部与眼神，背景机械设备自然虚化但轮廓清晰可辨。他额头的汗珠、皮肤的质感、工装上的油渍细节都清晰可见。背景机械带有真实的使用痕迹——锈斑、磨损、贴纸。写实纪实摄影风格，自然色调略带工业氛围的冷暖对比，高分辨率。

## English

**Worker Portrait in Industrial Plant**

A male worker in his 40s stands inside a large industrial plant, background featuring massive metal machinery and conveyor belt systems. He wears a dark blue work uniform, hands in pockets, face showing a tired but resolute expression. Fluorescent lights overhead cast cold white light, contrasting with natural warm light filtering through high windows from the side, creating split lighting effect on his face. Shot with 35mm f/2 lens, mid-shot composition, focus on the worker's face and eyes, background machinery naturally blurred but silhouettes clearly discernible. Sweat beads on his forehead, skin texture, oil stains on work uniform all clearly visible. Background machinery bears authentic signs of use—rust spots, wear, stickers. Realistic documentary photography style, natural tones with industrial atmosphere's cool-warm contrast, high resolution.
