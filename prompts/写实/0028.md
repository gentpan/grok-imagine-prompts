## 中文

**Then I handed the identical**

然后我将相同的素材集——三个图像参考(产品、变身前的女人和变身后的女人),没有开始帧,没有结束帧——加上相同的逐镜运动提示词和相同的组装方法,交给了大家现在正在争论的三个模型:Gemini Omni Flash、Kling 3.0 Omni和Seedance 2.0。

## English

**Then I handed the identical**

Then I handed the identical ingredient set — three image references (the product, the woman before her transformation, and the woman after it), no start frames, no end frames — plus the same shot-by-shot motion prompts and the same assembly method, to three of the models everyone is arguing about right now: Gemini Omni Flash, Kling 3.0 Omni, and Seedance 2.0.
