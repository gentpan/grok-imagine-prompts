## 中文

**专业摄影棚人像**

专业摄影棚内，一位 30 岁左右的亚洲女性模特正面半身肖像。她穿着简约的黑色高领毛衣，深色背景。采用 Rembrandt 光照设置：主光源从 45 度角照射，在面部一侧鼻梁下方形成标志性的三角形光斑。使用 85mm f/1.4 镜头，光圈全开营造奶油般的背景虚化效果，焦点精准落在眼睛上。模特表情自然放松，眼神直视镜头，展现专业而自信的气质。皮肤质感细腻真实，保留自然的毛孔与光影细节。色彩分级采用暖色调，略微降低饱和度，模仿中画幅胶片的色彩科学。专业级照片写实风格，高分辨率。

## English

**Professional Studio Portrait**

Inside a professional photography studio, frontal half-body portrait of an Asian female model around 30 years old. She wears a minimalist black turtleneck sweater against a dark background. Using Rembrandt lighting setup: key light from a 45-degree angle, creating the signature triangular highlight beneath the nose on one side of the face. Shot with an 85mm f/1.4 lens, aperture wide open to create creamy bokeh, focus precisely on the eyes. Model's expression is naturally relaxed, eyes looking directly at the camera, conveying professional and confident demeanor. Skin texture delicately realistic, preserving natural pores and light-shadow details. Color grading uses warm tones, slightly desaturated, mimicking medium format film color science. Professional-grade photorealistic style, high resolution.
