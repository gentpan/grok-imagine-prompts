## 中文

**I was moderated from uploading**

我因上传一张使用泥土填充桶和透视工具制作的地下房间图像而被审核。我要求Grok Imagine将其转换为Zebes风格的Boss战斗房间。没有生物,没有人类,没有色情内容。

## English

**I was moderated from uploading**

I was moderated from uploading an image I made using dirt fill buckets and perspective tools to make an underground room, and I asked Grok Imagine to turn it into a Zebes style boss fight room. No creatures, no humans, no porn.
