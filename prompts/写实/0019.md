## 中文

**I imagine most people arrive**

我想象大多数人来到M纯粹是出于个人原因。但最终你会到达一个点，你不仅意识到你所做的是更广阔事物的一部分——U的生命——而且与U的生命和谐相处是你之前认为仅仅是个人旅程的重要组成部分。

## English

**I imagine most people arrive**

I imagine most people arrive at M for strictly personal reasons. But eventually you reach a point where you not only realize that what you’re doing is part of something vastly larger—the Life of U—but that harmony with the Life of U is a vital component of what you previously thought of as just your own personal journey.
