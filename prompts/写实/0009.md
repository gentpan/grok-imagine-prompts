## 中文

**Psychedelic Gaze**

具有设计感的肖像摄影,迷幻的冷光蓝色调,蝴蝶光,年轻白人女性的特写镜头。她有着高级感的黑色短发,右侧眉毛上挑,浓密的睫毛,高鼻梁,咬着红唇,轻蔑地凝视着镜头。镜头拉远,前景是空中的碎玻璃,遮挡了女子的部分面部。

## English

**Psychedelic Gaze**

Portrait photography with a sense of design, psychedelic cold light blue tones, butterfly light, close-up shot of a young white woman. She has high-level short black hair, raised eyebrows on the right, thick eyelashes, high nose bridge, biting red lips, and staring disdainfully at the camera. The camera pulls back, and the foreground is broken glass in the air, which blocks part of the woman's face.
