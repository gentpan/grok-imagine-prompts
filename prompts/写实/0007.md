## 中文

**夜店自信**

一位年轻的成年女性站在室内夜店中，散发着自信，留着短短的黑色精灵头，身穿黑色文胸、豹纹贝雷帽、粗犷的氧化银项链、别致的戒指，披着羽毛披肩，身上有大胆的部落纹身。她的妆容大胆，闪光的眼影搭配哑光红唇，以中等特写镜头捕捉，使用平视角度的闪光灯摄影。光线温暖，带有柔和的琥珀色光晕，投射出精致的阴影，增强了忧郁而复古的2000年代氛围。背景中穿着鲜艳宝石色调服装的派对人群模糊呈现，增添了活力，整体采用温暖的棕色、金色、黑色和棕褐色调，3:4画幅比例，8K真实感。

## English

**Nightclub Confidence**

A young adult female in an indoor nightclub, exuding confidence with a short black pixie cut, wearing a black bralette, leopard beret, chunky oxidized silver necklace, eclectic rings, and a feather shawl with bold tribal tattoos. Her bold makeup features glittery eyelids and matte red lipstick, captured in a medium close-up with eye-level flash photography. The lighting is warm with soft amber glows, casting delicate shadows to enhance the moody, vintage 2000s atmosphere. Blurred partygoers in vibrant jewel-toned outfits in the background add liveliness, with warm browns, gold, black, and tan tones in a 3:4 aspect ratio, 8K realism.
