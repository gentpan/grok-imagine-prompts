## 中文

**Gothic Woman**

哥特风格女子的电影级肖像:苍白的皮肤、乌黑的头发,粗粝的真实感,环境光遮蔽,景深效果,黑白色调,她那锐利的眼神被戏剧性的眼线框住。

## English

**Gothic Woman**

Cinematic portrait of a gothic woman with pale skin and jet-black hair, abrasive authenticity, ambient occlusion, depth of field, black and white color palette, her piercing eyes framed by dramatic eyeliner.
