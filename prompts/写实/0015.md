## English

**I've been living in an**

I've been living in an apartment for almost 2 years now, currently with 4 others. One of our roommates, I'll nickname them "KC" (not their real initials), is 24M. KC has always been the least proactive out of all of us at cleaning up after themselves. KC would leave dirty dishes, cups, and pans in the kitchen, never washing them or putting them in the dishwasher unless we specifically asked him to. KC got a bloody nose once and didn't know what to do. I explained to him, quite relentlessly, to take a tissue and block their nose to keep any more blood from coming out. Somehow what he did instead was "fingerpaint" blood all over the bathroom and didn't mention nor clean it up. One of our other roommates found and cleaned it up but chose not to say anything to avoid any drama. KC got another bloody nose, this time he went into the kitchen and stood over the trash can for over 5 minutes blowing and picking his nose while dripping blood all over the kitchen floor. Afterwards, KC used some hand sanitizer then proceeded to make himself food, leaving a literal bloody mess in the kitchen which the rest of us cleaned up. KC did not mention to any of us about the mess. KC flooded the bathroom with his waste more than once by constantly flushing the toilet whenever it clogged instead of using the plunger. KC told our other roommates he could clean up the mess but he instead left the bathroom floor covered with waste for 3 hours until our roommates decided to clean it. The only thing they failed to do was unclog the toilet no matter how much they tried. KC reemerged to confess that he had flushed razor blades down the toilet which made our roommates understandably angry. Maintenance had to come in twice to fix the toilet, both times KC made himself scarce. KC had a major habit of helping himself to our food without asking or letting us know even when he had his own food. I made 2 pizzas for everyone and KC wordlessly took one entire pizza for himself. Another day I made a huge double batch of spaghetti and meatballs for the household, KC singlehandedly ate most of it before the rest of our roommates could have any. One of my roommates made a single pizza for their kid and only got 2 slices before KC stealthily took the rest. Another time, they made a pot of rice and within 2 minutes of it being done, KC quietly came out of his room and took 2/3 of the rice before anyone else got any. We would find milk, eggs, condiments, or frozen pizzas gone without notice or compensation so we resorted to labelling anything and everything by that point. Again, KC had his own food and was not starving by any means whatsoever. Even if he was, all he had to do was tell us and we'd happily share our food. KC started drinking heavily much to our surprise given he had previously said he avoided alcohol entirely. KC would be bringing either beer or vodka home and getting so intoxicated they'd be "swaying" in the kitchen barely able to stay standing while being unnaturally giggly, or he'd come home so drunk he'd unknowingly leave a "trail" of belongings from the front door to their room which one of our other roommates would kindly pick up for them. KC also started bringing home souvenir shot glasses. KC wrecked their bike and left it sitting by the front door for months. We thought he didn't have a way to transport it to a mechanic so I brought it to one to get fixed up. Afterwards, I mentioned it to KC along with the estimated $400 cost and he was okay with it, thankful even. When the bike was ready, KC went to go pick it up but came home without it because he "was under the assumption that" I was going to pay for it since he allegedly had only $60, which he had never once mentioned prior. The mechanic even tried to help by dropping the cost to $300 but that was still too much for KC. Some time passed and the mechanic was about to flag the bike as abandoned until KC magically pulled the money out of his butt and paid for the bike repairs. KC lost their job in autumn and seemingly struggled to find work, which was understandable given the job market is extremely rough. However, when I asked KC last month how the job search was going, they mentioned they "applied for a couple jobs but never heard back". KC continued to drink and play video games literally the entire day, never once contributing to household cleaning while the rest of us are gone at either work or school. KC let the rent and electricity bills go past due multiple times without even letting us know he hadn't paid their share until after the due date. Each and every time, KC did not say anything until prompted by one of us. First instance, I had to pay their share since he supposedly had no money at the time, though he did pay me back the following month. Second time, other roommates were short $5 on their share and asked KC if he could cover the rest. KC agreed and said he would login right away and pay the $5. The following morning I get a call from the manager regarding a remaining balance of $5 on our rent which KC apparently had not paid, so I went ahead and took care of it. Third time, KC once again waited until after rent was due to tell us he can't pay any rent for the month. We finally threatened to evict KC and they had a mental breakdown. KC got super drunk, paced around the apartment with shaky breaths while occasionally screaming into the void, he would try to initiate only to walk away when I acknowledged him. KC destroyed his smartphone he'd only had for maybe half a year by smashing and shattering the screen and leaving it on the bathroom counter. KC's behavior genuinely freaked me out especially since our other roommates had left to go out of town so it was just me and KC in the apartment. I ended up aimlessly driving around for 45 minutes just to be away from him. When I came back, KC had left a very drunkenly written apology note saying he did not know what he did to scare me out of the house. KC started to clean the kitchen more frequently which was highly unusual for him. He also claimed to have gotten a job, but he had "orientation" back in May and since then none of us have ever seen him actually be out of the house long enough for him to have worked an actual shift ever. He has only been out for maybe a couple hours at most in a day. Most days, KC is home in his room. We've begun to theorize KC once again isn't being honest with us and that he doesn't actually have a job in reality. I finally gave a date for KC to be out no later than. Since then, KC has once again stopped cleaning the kitchen except on rare occasions, and only doing a partial job at that. Our roommates have offered to help in ways they are able to, including letting KC use their storage unit. At this point, KC has lived here completely rent free for two rent cycles but his eviction date will arrive before the third rent bill. Our other roommates don't have any extra money to cover his rent so I've been covering the entirety of it. KC grew up with abusive narcissistic parents, which I also did so I can understand some of his pain, but I personally couldn't even imagine doing any of things KC has done, much less to the expense and detriment of our other roommates. Narcissistic parents often don't teach their children even basic life skills, so navigating life as an unexperienced adult can be especially difficult. Knowing this from personal experience, I and the others have tried to teach KC things, but it feels like everything goes in one ear and out the other with him. Add possible depression and/or crippling anxiety to that and it's a constant uphill battle it feels like you fight mostly on your own. I do feel sorry for KC and I wish them the best, but the rest of us are fed up with his lifestyle and other choices which are negatively impacting us emotionally and financially.
