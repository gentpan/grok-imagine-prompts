## 中文

**The production routing implication: if**

生产路由的含义:如果你不在提示词中写入摄像机取景,你会得到模型偏好的构图。Grok想向你展示动作——采用更宽的中景镜头,自然的手持式摄影机运动,强调动态姿态和环境背景。Seedance想向你展示细节——采用紧凑的特写取景,精心控制的工作室照明,突出材质纹理、皮肤细节和微妙的面部表情。光线方向、景深和相机角度的选择都反映了每个模型的内在美学偏好,形成了截然不同的视觉叙事风格。

## English

**The production routing implication: if**

The production routing implication: if you don't write camera framing into the prompt, you're getting the model's preferred composition. Grok wants to show you action—with wider mid-shot framing, natural handheld camera motion, emphasizing dynamic poses and environmental context. Seedance wants to show you detail—with tight close-up framing, carefully controlled studio lighting, highlighting material textures, skin details, and subtle facial expressions. The choice of light direction, depth of field, and camera angle all reflect each model's inherent aesthetic preference, creating distinctly different visual narrative styles.
