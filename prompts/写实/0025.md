## 中文

**街头纪实摄影**

黄昏时分的城市街道，一位年轻人背着背包穿过斑马线的照片级真实场景。夕阳的金色光线从建筑物之间投射下来，在湿润的路面上形成长长的影子和反光。使用35mm f/2.0镜头，街头摄影构图，捕捉到人物行走的自然动态瞬间。背景有模糊的车辆和行人，增强景深层次感。色彩真实，对比度适中，呈现出纪实摄影的质感。颗粒感细腻，仿佛使用胶片相机拍摄。充满生活气息的城市氛围，自然光影效果。

## English

**Street Documentary Photography**

A photorealistic scene at dusk on a city street, capturing a young person with a backpack crossing a zebra crossing. Golden hour sunlight streams between buildings, casting long shadows and reflections on the wet pavement surface. Shot with a 35mm f/2.0 lens, street photography composition, capturing the natural dynamic moment of walking. Blurred vehicles and pedestrians in the background add depth and layers. Authentic colors with moderate contrast, conveying documentary photography texture. Fine grain structure as if shot on film camera. Natural lighting effects with urban atmosphere full of everyday life. Realistic weather conditions, ambient street lighting beginning to illuminate, creating a transitional mood between day and night.
