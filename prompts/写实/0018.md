## 中文

**咖啡馆窗边沉思**

一位 25 岁左右的女性坐在复古咖啡馆靠窗位置，侧脸注视着窗外的雨景。她身穿米色针织开衫，一手托腮，另一手轻握白瓷咖啡杯。窗外模糊的雨滴在玻璃上形成抽象光点，雨后街道的霓虹灯色彩倒映在她的侧脸上。采用 50mm f/1.8 镜头，浅景深，焦点在她的眼睛与睫毛细节。咖啡馆内部光线柔和温暖，来自窗外的自然光与室内暖黄色吊灯形成自然混合照明。皮肤质感真实，可见细腻的绒毛与自然光影过渡。背景中模糊的咖啡馆座位与顾客轮廓营造空间感。写实摄影风格，自然色调，电影级色彩分级，高分辨率细节。

## English

**Contemplation by the Cafe Window**

A woman around 25 years old sits by the window in a vintage cafe, profile view gazing at the rain outside. She wears a beige knit cardigan, one hand supporting her chin, the other lightly holding a white porcelain coffee cup. Outside the window, blurred raindrops form abstract light spots on the glass, and neon lights from the rain-soaked street reflect on her profile. Shot with 50mm f/1.8 lens, shallow depth of field, focus on her eye and eyelash details. The cafe's interior lighting is soft and warm, natural light from the window mixing with warm yellow pendant lamps inside. Realistic skin texture showing fine facial hair and natural light-shadow transitions. Blurred cafe seating and customer silhouettes in the background create spatial depth. Photorealistic photography style, natural color tones, cinematic color grading, high-resolution details.
