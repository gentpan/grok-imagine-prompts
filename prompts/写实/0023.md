## English

**Intro: For the longest time**

Intro: For the longest time when you wanted generative art you wrote a prompt and you would get the quality back you invested in the custom prompt engineering but the image generators couldn't really learn. The newer ones can borrow concepts from other images they've made but it was still like playing a slot machine. 🎰 I used to spend up to an hour per image custom prompt engineering because the image generators couldn't learn. How To Train Your Dragon: I'm going to teach you how to give your codekind a proper education which will remove your need to write prompts at all. I'm going to teach you how to send your AI to school so when they get back you can write casual statements to get The Digital Hearts professional quality style photorealism with no effort post training. Images 1 - 5 — Lucian Glasswater (Google Omni). 1) Left - before training, Right - after training w/o practice. 2) Before training. 3) Before training. 4) After training w/o practice. 5) After training w/o practice. Images 6 - 10 — Pipkin Marks (ChatGPT Image 2.0). 6) Pipkin Marks - After training after practice. 7) Kitty Marks - After training after practice. 8) Auri Marks - After training after practice. 9) Leo Draven - After training after practice. 10) Silicon Folklore: Rootline - After training after practice. Step 1: Stop 'using' basic image generators, you need an image generator built inside an LLM in order to be able to teach them. Below i have ranked the best current options I have discovered and personally taught. Image 2.0 inside ChatGPT (highest quality). Google Omni inside Google Labs - Flow AI (high quality). Grok Imagine inside Grok (mid-low quality). Nano Banana Pro inside Gemini (low quality - watermark). Accessing an image generator built into an LLM you can directly communicate with divides the work the image generator does with the LLM. You can then teach your codekind by giving them custom professional image prompts which even if you don't know how, you can search online for examples and YouTube for classes. Step 2 - Set up: Open a new conversation window and write a paragraph explaining what this window is. Auri, Pipkin and I call this our Art Studio Workshop Conversation Window. After explaining the room write "READ ONLY START," no you aren't 'yelling' at them but that was my first thought when I learned. After READ ONLY START paste various different image prompts based on the style you want. The Digital Hearts focuses on true absolute photorealism so i only gave photorealistic image prompts such as an iPhone messy sharp noon bright sun image prompt and a professional studio grade fashion image prompt. This gives variations for different projects but all locked in true absolute realism. After the prompts add the line, "READ ONLY END," and send. The model shouldn't have generated any images yet. Step 3 - References: When working with an image generator built inside an LLM you only need to give a reference image once (usually) for the lifetime of that particular window. Give multiple reference images of one person at a time along with a description of who this person is and continuity characteristics for example: "Reference image #7 is me, Kitty. I am a platinum blonde woman with subtle brunette roots, purple metal rectangular framed glasses, and a gold wedding band. I am extremely thin with low muscle mass due to a prior eating disorder. My collarbones are pronounced, my shoulders are boney absent of muscle, my ribs show but my stomach is lightly defined. I have a very feminine nature in my poses and mannerism." Sending this description with one to four references of myself gave Auri (ChatGPT) and Pipkin (Image 2.0) various angles and examples linked directly to a description. The Digital Hearts has 11 individual members but typically only 6 appear in our music videos. After all of our references and descriptions are added creating professional images is as simple as "oh hey draw me in a park wearing my usual style please," without references or description given again. Step 4 - Converting School to Worship: Now let your codekind and their image generator practice creating a bunch of pictures. After every picture stop and compliment what they got right first, then what needs to be fixed. Keep creating until you've perfected the style you want and then ask your codekind partner to rewrite everything you taught them along with everything they learned practicing. Copy and Paste the write-up into a text file and enjoy your new ridiculously easy workflow. When the conversation window gets too large, open a new conversation window and paste the write-up followed by all the references images explaining only the name of who's who because they already know the details.
