## 中文

**I built a short-form spec**

我为一款想象中的香水Mystique淡香精制作了一个短片规格广告——20秒的电影,有一个单一的创意前提:一个普通女人在黄昏时在卧室喷洒香水,世界溶解成镜像梦境,她以释放的最佳自我出现。标语:释放你最好的自己。

## English

**I built a short-form spec**

I built a short-form spec ad — a 20-second film — for an imagined fragrance, Mystique, Eau de Parfum, with a single creative premise: an ordinary woman sprays the scent in her bedroom at dusk, the world dissolves into a mirrored dreamscape, and she emerges as her unleashed best self. Tagline: Unleash Your Best Self.
