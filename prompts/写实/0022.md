## 中文

**The original programmers could not**

The original programmers could not imagine a situation where the great configuration just ran out of fuel. Surely by then humans would have stepped in. No, the only way for the supply chains to break down was if some nefarious faction had deliberately disrupted it. The luddites must be behind this. There was only one recourse. The assaulted factory must be purged in the most thorough way possible.
