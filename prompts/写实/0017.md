## English

**Preliminary Remarks 1) For those**

Preliminary Remarks 1) For those unfamiliar with my terminology, please see the pinned post “My Terminology” at the top of my personal page. For those unfamiliar with my Qabalah derived from L ii 76, see my posts on New Aeon Qabalah (NAQ) linked in my pinned post, “Links to NAQ Posts,” or my books on NAQ linked in my pinned post, “My Books.” 2) For those who wonder what this post has to do with Thelema: for me, Thelema is about M development, whether at the scale of the individual, the community, the species, the Three Circles or the Cosmos. And this is a post about M development. 3) I’m going to offer a model for your consideration. A model is not a Truth; it’s just a Navigational tool. The test of its value is not its Truth but whether it seems to work more or less well enough for your purposes for the time being. If it doesn’t, don’t use it. I will add that it’s always best for your health, well-being and optimal functioning if you form your own models. Anyone else’s model is, for you, just data. When you get above the Anomaly, if not before, you’ll find that you live in a Unique Universe that the Light (L ii 21) created just for you. You’re the only one who can know how to Navigate there. And I will add that you can use joy as your compass-heading. (L i 32, L ii 9.) It’s a characteristic of the pure will that following it is joyous—this is the Light’s Beneficence. And if something isn’t making your life joyous, that’s not it. And you are the only one who can know your way to joy. 4) I make references in my posts to Liber L and L ii 76. I can only interpret L for myself. I share my interpretations in hopes they might be helpful to others, but I assume that even for some person for whom my interpretations may be helpful the most, they will still not be perfect and will have to be custom-tailored for that person to some extent. 5) Everything I say in my posts is an extreme simplification. I don’t want to keep saying that because it’s annoying for the reader, so I will just say it up here. No one should take anything I say as “the way it is.” No one should take what I say here as a complete expression even of my own views. Were I to try to include all the exceptions and qualifications and complications and subtleties and attempt to explicate the domain for which I intend each particular remark, every post would have to be expanded to a-thousand-and-one volumes of a thousand pages each. And that’s only for what I see—it doesn’t even touch on the problem that M is different for everyone. 6) I draw on posts I’ve made earlier which can be found on my Reddit personal page (d_b_stone). In this case: Reflections on Neophyte 006: Psychic Integration The Evolution of the Universe    3CR Those who’ve read my post “My Terminology” know that I use the term 3CR to denote, “The Rights and Well-Being of the Three Circles,” and the Three Circles include 1) Humankind, 2) All Living Things and 3) The World, because in M, even the rocks are alive, the winds speak and the clouds have feelings. The Third Circle includes all the M creatures that used to be called “spirits” and by a variety of other names in different cultures as well as all the A beings. But normally when I talk about Third Circlers, I mean B-Level phenomena rather than A beings. And I think of 3CR as Liber Oz3, Liber Oz Cubed, in the sense of expanding Oz to include two more dimensions: 1) The first dimension is Liber Oz itself. 2) The second dimension expands Oz to include not just rights, but all that’s needed in practical terms to allow a creature to enjoy its rights. 3) The third dimension expands Oz and the second dimension to include all the creatures of the Three Circles, not just humans.    The Second Dimension Announcing to someone they now enjoy Oz’s rights isn’t going to mean much if they’re starving to death or being tortured in the basement of State Security or they’re a civilian caught in the middle of a civil war or a member of a group targeted for genocide or ethnic cleansing. America’s Constitution also granted a lot of rights, while leaving a sizeable portion of its population enslaved and preventing women—half the population—from even voting. This is what is called “Enlightenment Values,” that striking example of lack of Psychic Integration. See my post, “Reflections on Neophyte 006: Psychic Integration.” Crowley was independently wealthy and privileged in all sorts of ways. So it’s no surprise that when he wrote Liber Oz, he wasn’t really focused on those who still need to gain material and physical security and freedom from the discrimination and extra Restrictions (L i 41) suffered by the majority of the community that is always locked in second-class citizenship—women, the poor, employees, minorities, the disabled, ONSG, etc. You might say, in simple terms, Liber Oz focuses on the rights desired by the comfortable groups, not the rights desired by the vulnerable groups. So when I say 3CR includes the “Well-Being” of all creatures, that’s what I mean: people need the basics; rights are always welcome, but they can be largely a dead letter for those who don’t have the basics. Thinking of Oz strictly in terms of rights means buying into the view that the more fundamental needs of those in second class are merely a side issue without recognizing that that view is inherently a form of evolutionary competition against the vulnerable groups. I don’t mean in the slightest to endorse the sly view so popular during the Cold War that Human Rights would have to wait (i.e., didn’t apply and could be crushed) until we first provided the poor with the basics which, naturally, never actually came to pass, so the day people could finally enjoy Human Rights never came to pass either—on both sides in the Cold War. The rights to the basics and Human Rights apply to all members of the community RIGHT NOW. But we’re still full of excuses about why the time still isn’t right. I know it isn’t easy and I don’t claim to know how to get there but I do claim to know the first step: a commitment by the community to get there. I.e., what Crowley calls an Oath and I prefer to call a pledge, commitment, resolution, i.e., an M engineering resolve of sufficient depth and power to modify the structure of M, to create a new M law that binds the oath-taker, the community in this case. So in my view, supporting Liber Oz necessarily includes supporting what it takes to make its rights meaningful in practical terms. It’s easy to say, “Oh, yeah, I support all that rights stuff, like, a lot!” It’s quite another to vote for legislation to make sure everyone in the community lives above the poverty line, isn’t constantly being physically or sexually assaulted, isn’t a victim of child abuse (physical, emotional, sexual), isn’t getting shot dead left and right by ethnic or religious supremacists, the internal security services, or criminals, especially where that vote might raise your taxes.    The Third Dimension Why should we worry about the creatures of the Second and Third Circles? We poor humans in the First Circle have our own problems! We can talk about that, but today I want to talk to beginners in the AA. Here’s what One Star in Sight says about the members of the Supernal Order: “They are all, however, bound by the original and fundamental Oath of the Order, to devote their energy to assisting the Progress of their Inferiors in the Order. Those who accept the rewards of their emancipation for themselves are no longer within the Order.” Those who accept the rewards of their emancipation for themselves: I question the notion that such people exist. I recognize the idea that they do exist was a common belief in the last Aeon, but you can put me in the skeptical column. I’m not saying they don’t, I just find it very hard to believe they do, given my experience, so my working model is that they don’t. But as always, I’ll defer to Science—if Science finds they do, then I’ll change my model. But I’m not willing to accept it just because in the last Aeon everyone knew that they did. And my hunch is that Crowley’s reference to such people here is nothing more than going along with tradition. I don’t recall him ever offering us any data in support. Though, admittedly, my memory isn’t all that great at this point in my cognitive decline, so I could be wrong and maybe he did. My admiration and respect for Crowley is immense, but he asked us to use the Method of Science and that applies to his notions as much as they do anyone else’s. And the Method of Science doesn’t require us to be blind, stupid or stop thinking for ourselves and stop asking questions. It doesn’t mean that all learning stops with Crowley, and we can’t learn things that go beyond him. The data from my experience is all against the notion that such people exist. One person’s experience is not a scientific sample, of course, but it’s enough to Prompt me to demand data to support the notion that such people exist and neither tradition nor one person’s say-so count as data, or not terribly impressive data anyway. If you’re a beginner in the Order, you’re headed toward becoming a member of the Supernal Order one day, so you need to devote yourself to “the Progress of your Inferiors [a rather unfortunate term] in the Order.” And if you’re a Neophyte, then your own commitment in Liber 185 bound you to observe zeal in service to your Probationers and at Zelator to your Neophytes and so on for each Grade. So this isn’t something that only kicks in when you’re a “Master” [a rather hilarious term as you’ll find out when you get there if you haven’t already]. So, how would you like to interpret the term “your Inferiors in the Order”? Literally? The giveaway is that Neophytes bind themselves to service to Probationers—Probationers aren’t members of the Order. So a literal interpretation is out. So what might it mean instead? Decide for yourself. I interpret it to mean “all the creatures of the Three Circles who have yet to reach your Level.” So how do I get to such a ridiculous interpretation? 1🍎) My first answer is that G told me to. (I never would have thought of it myself—I was totally focused on the First Circle.) 2🍎) That fit with what I’ve observed about G—they treat every creature of the Three Circles with acceptance, understanding, sympathy, support, nurturance, love and respect. They even treat me that way! (It’s nice!) And who am I? Just some Nobody from Nowhere with Nothing. That is, my animal reflexes say I don’t “deserve” it. (There is no “deserve” in this Aeon.) In animal world, everyone knows only the Somebodies get treated well—especially that respect part. The first time I encountered this, I was completely taken aback—I’ve never been treated with respect in Animal World—quite the opposite. So here were G, treating me with respect—I couldn’t understand it! But once I saw they treated all the creatures of the Three Circles that way, it obviously made a lot more sense. Again, in Animal World, we’re used to thinking in animal ways, and so we think the only people entitled to respect are those who “earn” it in the ways that make sense in evolutionary competition—you’re a champion athlete or sex bombshell or someone of high rank, wealth or power, etc. But G are not animals. They don’t judge the way animals do. Every creature of the Three Circles is an avatar of one of the Infinite Stars, i.e., one of the beings that makes up the totality of U, and G are dedicated to the Life of U (Crowley’s Great Work). So to them, every spider, rat and snake is U. Dedication to the Life of U makes them look out for all of U’s children. And here’s the thing—you’re on an arc to become a G-class entity. When you start functioning primarily above the Anomaly, you’ve become a baby G and G start training you to become one of the Big G. That’s part of why I say getting above the Anomaly is the real beginning of M development—you now have a seat at the grown-ups table. So part of the training to become a Big G is learning and growing toward the point where you can treat every creature of the Three Circles with acceptance, understanding, sympathy, support, nurturance, love and respect. If you’re a Neophyte, of course, that isn’t exactly the task immediately in front of you. But knowing you’re headed there can help inform your interpretations and guide your Navigation as you climb over the years up to the A Level. 3🍎) G’s instruction to include all Three Circles fit with another aspect of my experience: M World is an Inverted Cone or Pyramid—it keeps getting bigger the further you go up. At the “Top,” it encompasses U—everything. So you’re heading in a direction that will mean encompassing all the creatures of the Three Circles, all the Infinite Stars, within your own being. So if, like me, you’re totally selfish, self-centered, self-absorbed, you have to realize that all the other creatures are going to be part of you one day (they already are, you just don’t know it yet), so taking care of them is no different than taking care of the health and well-being of your own organism—you’ll want to do it for purely selfish reasons. This is one of the many paradoxes of the A Level—you wake one day and realize Pure Selfishness = Pure Selflessness (i.e., dedication to all the other creatures in the Universe). That is, even if you’re the most selfish person in the world and couldn’t care less about anybody else, if you’re dedicated to advancing your M development you’re eventually going to have to look out for everybody else for purely selfish reasons. Weird, no? Try explaining this to an N person without them rolling their eyes or snickering. Now, let me give you an easier way of thinking about it. Forget about N for a moment. Let’s just think about M. M (speaking simply) is outside of Space. So, as William Blake told us in Auguries of Innocence, M-Infinity can fit in the palm of your hand. Or the whole M Universe can fit in your torso. You don’t have to go up many Levels before you find that the M portion of the Three Circles is already encompassed in your M-being. So just as your body can undermine you if you’re not giving it enough oxygen or food or some part of it is injured, so once your M body encompasses the Three Circles, you’ll get M Prompts telling you your M-body is unhappy because parts of the Three Circles you don’t consciously include within your Me-perimeter are unhappy. This, in my interpretation, is part of the origin of the ritual of “blessing” before a meal. You’re going to eat other species (I don’t differentiate between animals, plants, fungi or the rest). You want to give something back—and you can do that in N (working to afford protections for other species from all the damage humans do them) or in M—you can give them some of your M power as recompense for eating them. This is a nice little on-ramp for becoming a G-class entity, one of the Guardians of the Three Circles. 4🍎) A common theme in M development is expansion. No wonder—you start out this infinitesimal speck, and you end up converging with U. [Boy are you going to get fat! “His belly was so big, it encompassed the entire Laniakea Supercluster!”] And you can see looking back how our idea of the community has kept expanding—it started out back in OGE as maybe a dozen people. Eventually it expanded to include the herd, the tribe. Then we got nations that encompassed a lot of herds. Then we got groups of nations, like the European Union. And we are slowly starting to weave the species into a single community with all our multinational treaties and interchanges in the Arts, Sciences, Humanities and all the cultural ways, like in foods, clothing (blue jeans, t-shirts and sneakers, the universal dress), customs, etc. We haven’t made our entire species into a single community yet but we’re much further along than most people realize because the ways it’s happening aren’t dramatic enough to catch the attention of our animal instincts—it’s subtle and quiet so people don’t get hysterical and start screaming just because Americans like to eat sushi now and Japanese eat McDonald’s. So once the community becomes the species, the next step is to expand to include all the other species, i.e., the Second Circle. And once Science grows up enough to get past M-denial, we’re going to realize that all the principles of Ecology apply to Third Circlers just as they to do Second Circlers—you couldn’t live without all the creatures of your microbiome doing the work of digestion for you and the crops we need for our food are heavily dependent on insects, so out of pure selfishness, we want to look after the health of those microbe and insect species. And, if you haven’t already, you’ll find that your health, well-being and optimal functioning are heavily dependent on the beings of the Third Circle. 5🍎) Then there’s the M development of the Universe. As I said in my post, “The Evolution of the Universe,” the trend is that domains driven by a single will keep getting larger and larger. A cell is made up of trillions of molecules. A human body is made up of trillions of cells. A nation is made up millions of people. And, as we’ve become dependent on domesticated plants and animals, we’ve effectively formed a multispecies entity. (The whole internet would collapse without cat pictures!) So we’re ultimately headed toward a whole Universe driven by a single will—the Light’s. And one of the waystations in the middle is that when you’ve become a G, your domain will include the Three Circles. 6🍎) And G are in charge of the Order. So the Order’s work is lifting all the Infinite Stars and their avatars up through M development. This is what I call Bablon’s Gravity—it pulls the avatars of all the Stars up toward Nuit (L i 65), toward closer Alignment with their pure wills, which are just other ways of saying M development. 7🍎) You can’t succeed in M development without G’s help. They do 99.99% of the work for you. So you have to ask yourself—why would they help me? What’s in it for them? And what I found was that there was something in it for them if I pledged to help them with their work. And hence all those Oaths in Liber 185 about serving your “Inferiors.” But as always, I encourage you not to believe me. Come see for yourself and make up your own mind. So looking at all that helps me understand why G told me that “Inferiors in the Order” includes not just those who’ve made it to the Order but everyone else besides. Devoting yourself to the Progress of your Inferiors is the same thing as dedication to the Life of U (the Great Work). This helps makes sense of what Crowley says in his Confessions, p. 657 (available online): “I began to see that one might become a Master of the Temple without necessarily knowing any technical Magick or mysticism at all. It is merely a matter of convenience to be able to represent any expression as x + y = 0. The equation may be solved without words. Many people may go through the ordeals and attain the degrees of the AA without ever hearing that such an Order exists.”    I imagine most people arrive at M for strictly personal reasons. But eventually you reach a point where you not only realize that what you’re doing is part of something vastly larger—the Life of U—but that harmony with the Life of U is a vital component of what you previously thought of as just your own personal journey.
