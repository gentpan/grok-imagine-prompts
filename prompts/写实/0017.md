## 中文

**思想者肖像**

一位 50 多岁的学者坐在书房的皮革扶手椅上，手指交叉托着下巴，陷入深思。他穿着深棕色羊毛开衫，背景是满墙的书架，古旧书籍整齐排列。窗外柔和的自然光从侧面照射进来，在他脸部形成 Rembrandt 光照效果，一侧脸颊明亮，另一侧隐入阴影。采用 85mm f/1.8 镜头，半身肖像构图，焦点精准落在他的眼睛与眉间皱纹细节上。皮肤质感真实，可见岁月留下的细纹、斑点与自然的光影过渡。背景书架自然虚化，但书脊上的文字依然隐约可辨。桌上放着翻开的笔记本与钢笔。整体氛围沉静而富有智慧感，暖色调，复古学院风格，高分辨率。

## English

**Portrait of a Thinker**

A scholar in his 50s sits in a leather armchair in his study, fingers interlaced supporting his chin, deep in thought. He wears a dark brown wool cardigan, background filled with bookshelves, old books neatly arranged. Soft natural light from the window illuminates from the side, creating Rembrandt lighting effect on his face, one cheek bright, the other fading into shadow. Shot with 85mm f/1.8 lens, half-body portrait composition, focus precisely on his eyes and details of wrinkles between brows. Realistic skin texture showing fine lines left by time, spots, and natural light-shadow transitions. Background bookshelves naturally blurred, but text on book spines still faintly discernible. On the desk lies an open notebook and fountain pen. Overall atmosphere is quiet and intellectual, warm tones, vintage academic style, high resolution.
