## 中文

**专业人像摄影**

一位中年女性的照片级写实肖像，柔和的窗户自然光从左侧45度角照射，营造出细腻的光影过渡。背景虚化的摄影工作室，使用85mm f/1.4镜头拍摄，浅景深效果突出人物主体。皮肤纹理清晰可见，眼神专注而自然，发丝细节丰富。色调温暖真实，后期轻度调色保持照片真实感。高分辨率、专业人像摄影风格。

## English

**Professional Portrait Photography**

A photorealistic portrait of a middle-aged woman, illuminated by soft natural window light coming from a 45-degree angle on the left, creating delicate shadow transitions and dimensional depth. Blurred photography studio background. Shot with an 85mm f/1.4 lens, shallow depth of field emphasizing the subject. Visible skin texture with natural pores and fine lines, focused and natural eye expression, rich hair detail with individual strands visible. Warm and authentic color grading, minimal post-processing to maintain photographic realism. High resolution, professional portrait photography style, studio lighting setup.
