## 中文

**Vanity Glow**

一幅电影感的肖像，一位年轻的东亚女性优雅地坐在白色大理石梳妆台前。她有着长长的、光滑的深棕色头发，中分造型简单地修饰着她的脸庞。她的肌肤光洁细腻，带有温暖的色调。她化着柔和的桃色妆容，眼睑上有淡淡的闪光，飞扬的眼线和珊瑚色口红赋予她精致的光彩。她身穿露肩的黑色天鹅绒连衣裙，突显出颈部和肩膀的线条，优雅而现代。一条精致的银色项链搭配小吊坠垂在锁骨处，她还佩戴着配套的银色耳环和戒指，增添了精致的气质。她的姿态自信而女性化，一只手臂自然地放在大理石台面上，另一只手臂放松地垂在身侧。场景设置在柔和灯光照射的梳妆空间中，镜子映照出她部分的剪影。光线均衡而柔和，营造出干净、奢华的外观，柔和的阴影，没有刺眼的高光，肌肤质感光滑。梳妆台上摆放着化妆品，如粉饼盘、小巧的装饰刷和整齐摆放在她身边的粉底液瓶。背景是深海军蓝或深色窗帘，极简主义装饰，包括装满橙色、奶油色和金色等温暖秋季色调干花的白色花瓶。整体氛围优雅、沉稳且低调奢华，如同用清晰的现代相机拍摄的高端美容社论，配以电影级照明和8K超真实清晰度。色彩方案：象牙白、黑色天鹅绒、温暖的桃色调和柔和的金色花卉点缀。画幅比例：4:5。风格：超写实、奢华肖像、杂志美学。

## English

**Vanity Glow**

A cinematic portrait of a young East Asian woman sitting gracefully at a white marble vanity table. She has long, sleek, dark brown hair parted in the middle, styled simply to frame her face. Her skin is luminous and smooth with a warm tone. She wears soft, peach-toned makeup with a light shimmer on the eyelids, winged eyeliner, and coral lipstick that gives her a polished glow. She’s dressed in an off-shoulder black velvet dress that accentuates her neckline and shoulders, elegant yet modern. A delicate silver necklace with a small pendant rests on her collarbone, and she wears matching silver earrings and a ring, adding a refined touch. Her posture is confident and feminine, one arm resting naturally on the marble surface, the other relaxed by her side. The scene takes place in a softly lit vanity space with a mirror reflecting part of her silhouette. The lighting is balanced and diffused, creating a clean, luxurious look, soft shadows, no harsh highlights, and a smooth skin texture. On the vanity, there are makeup items such as a compact palette, a small decorative brush, and bottles of foundation neatly placed beside her. The background features deep navy or dark curtains and minimalist decor, including white vases filled with dried flowers in warm autumn hues of orange, cream, and gold. The overall mood feels elegant, poised, and quietly glamorous, like a high-end beauty editorial captured on a crisp modern camera with cinematic lighting and 8K ultra-realistic clarity. Color palette: ivory white, black velvet, warm peach tones, and soft gold floral accents. Aspect ratio: 4:5. Style: hyper-realistic, luxury portrait, magazine aesthetic.
