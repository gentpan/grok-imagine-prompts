## 中文

**KC grew up with abusive**

KC在虐待性自恋父母的环境中长大,我也是如此,所以我能理解他的一些痛苦,但我个人根本无法想象做KC所做的任何事情,更不用说以牺牲和损害我们其他室友为代价了。

## English

**KC grew up with abusive**

KC grew up with abusive narcissistic parents, which I also did so I can understand some of his pain, but I personally couldn't even imagine doing any of things KC has done, much less to the expense and detriment of our other roommates.
