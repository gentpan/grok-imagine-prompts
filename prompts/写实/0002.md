## 中文

**Misty Graveyard**

Photorealistic snapshot of a drizzly autumn morning in a remote coastal graveyard: weathered granite headstones leaning slightly amid overgrown grass and brambles, sea mist rolling in from the horizon; a solitary crow perches on a rusted iron fence post, head cocked unnaturally still, wings half-unfurled as if listening—gentle bokeh on dew-kissed spiderwebs in the foreground, desaturated grays and muted browns, no dramatic shadows or supernatural flares.
