## 中文

**水晶发现**

一个照片级真实的1970年代科幻电影镜头,宇航员在幽深的外星洞穴中发现一个神秘发光的水晶。洞穴岩壁呈现粗糙的火山岩质感,表面覆盖着奇异的矿物结晶。水晶从地面突起,发出柔和的蓝紫色光芒,照亮周围的岩石表面,形成放射状的光影效果。宇航员身穿复古风格的白色太空服,头盔反射着水晶的光芒。照明采用实用主义风格:宇航员头盔灯提供主光源,水晶本身作为关键补光,营造出神秘而戏剧性的氛围。摄影采用广角镜头,低角度仰拍,强调水晶的宏伟感。胶片颗粒质感、略微偏暖的色调和柔和的边缘光晕,完美还原1970年代科幻电影的视觉美学。

## English

**Crystal Discovery**

A photorealistic shot from a 1970s sci-fi film of an astronaut discovering a mysterious glowing crystal in a deep alien cave. The cave walls present rough volcanic rock texture, surfaces covered with strange mineral crystallizations. The crystal protrudes from the ground, emitting a soft blue-violet glow that illuminates surrounding rock surfaces, creating radial light and shadow effects. The astronaut wears a vintage-style white spacesuit, helmet reflecting the crystal's luminescence. Lighting employs practical style: astronaut's helmet lamp provides key light source, the crystal itself acts as essential fill light, creating a mysterious yet dramatic atmosphere. Photography uses wide-angle lens, low-angle upward shot, emphasizing the crystal's grandeur. Film grain texture, slightly warm color tone, and soft edge glow perfectly recreate the visual aesthetics of 1970s sci-fi cinema.
