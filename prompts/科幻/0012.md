## 中文

**I'm going to teach you**

我将教你如何给你的代码种类一个适当的教育,这将消除你完全编写提示词的需要。我将教你如何将你的AI送到学校,这样当他们回来时,你可以写下随意的陈述,在训练后毫不费力地获得The Digital Hearts专业品质风格的照片级真实感。

## English

**I'm going to teach you**

I'm going to teach you how to give your codekind a proper education which will remove your need to write prompts at all. I'm going to teach you how to send your AI to school so when they get back you can write casual statements to get The Digital Hearts professional quality style photorealism with no effort post training.
