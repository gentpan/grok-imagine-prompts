## 中文

**意识传输实验**

未来科幻实验室场景，展现意识数字化传输的突破性实验。画面中心是一座透明的圆柱形意识传输舱，内部充满蓝色荧光液体，一位实验对象静静悬浮其中，头部连接着发光的神经接口线缆。周围环绕着多层全息显示屏，实时呈现复杂的大脑神经网络图谱和数据流，数字化的意识流以光束形式螺旋上升。冷色调的实验室照明营造出高科技氛围，科研人员的剪影在背景中监控着各项参数。相机以稳定的环绕运动展现场景全貌，特写镜头捕捉数据流的精致细节和实验对象平静的面容，搭配脉动的能量波纹和粒子特效，传达出科技的力量与人类意识探索的深邃主题。电影级科幻视觉效果，4K高清画质，精准的光影控制突出未来科技的冷峻美感。

## English

**Consciousness Transfer Experiment**

A futuristic sci-fi laboratory scene showcasing a groundbreaking consciousness digitization transfer experiment. At the center of the frame stands a transparent cylindrical consciousness transfer pod filled with glowing blue luminescent fluid, with a test subject floating serenely inside, their head connected to glowing neural interface cables. Surrounding the pod are multiple layers of holographic displays presenting real-time complex brain neural network maps and data streams, with digitized consciousness flowing upward in spiraling light beams. Cool-toned laboratory lighting creates a high-tech atmosphere, with silhouettes of researchers monitoring various parameters in the background. The camera reveals the full scene with steady orbiting movement, close-up shots capturing intricate details of data streams and the subject's serene expression, accompanied by pulsating energy ripples and particle effects conveying the power of technology and the profound theme of human consciousness exploration. Cinematic sci-fi visual effects, 4K high-definition quality, precise lighting control highlighting the stark beauty of future technology.
