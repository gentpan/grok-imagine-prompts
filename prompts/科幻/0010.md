## 中文

**In the sprawling world of**

在广阔的Militsioner世界中，规模改变一切。当城市的霓虹动脉随生命跳动时，一个庞大警察的存在不是恐惧的遗迹，而是秩序与混乱之间的活生生的界限。这位不懈的守护者，以纪念碑般的规模想象出来，重新定义了玩家对监控、安全以及保护与偏执之间细微界限的认知。

## English

**In the sprawling world of**

In the sprawling world of Militsioner, scale changes everything. When the city’s neon arteries pulse with life, the presence of a colossal policeman looms not as a relic of fear, but as a living boundary between order and chaos. This relentless guardian, imagined at a monumental scale, redefines how players perceive surveillance, safety, and the thin line between protection and paranoia.
