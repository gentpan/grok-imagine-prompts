## 中文

**雨夜小巷**

一个中景镜头,跟随一个孤独的人物在夜晚穿过雨中的赛博朋克小巷。湿漉漉的路面如同黑色镜面,闪烁着粉色、蓝色和绿色的霓虹灯倒影,光线在雨滴和水坑中折射出梦幻般的光晕。照明来自悬挂的日式广告牌、店铺橱窗和蒸汽管道的暖光,营造出强烈的明暗对比。雨雾弥漫在空气中,柔化了背景的建筑轮廓。摄影采用手持拍摄的胶片感,35毫米镜头,轻微的颗粒质感和自然的摄影机晃动,色温偏冷,增强了潮湿、忧郁而充满未来感的氛围。景深适中,突出主体同时保留环境细节。

## English

**Rainy Alley**

A medium shot following a solitary figure walking through a rainy cyberpunk alley at night. The wet pavement acts like a black mirror, glistening with pink, blue, and green neon reflections, light refracting through raindrops and puddles creating dreamlike halos. Lighting comes from hanging Japanese-style billboards, storefront windows, and warm glow from steam pipes, creating strong chiaroscuro contrast. Rain mist permeates the air, softening the architectural silhouettes in the background. Photography employs handheld film aesthetic, 35mm lens, subtle grain texture and natural camera shake, cool color temperature, enhancing the damp, melancholic yet futuristic atmosphere. Medium depth of field highlights the subject while preserving environmental details.
