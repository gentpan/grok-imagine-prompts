## 中文

**Glowing Cube**

一个发光的未来主义立方体,带有精密的蓝色发光网格线和脉动的橙色能量光效,悬浮在镜面般的反射表面上方。立方体表面呈现半透明的全息材质,内部可见复杂的几何结构和流动的数据流。照明采用冷暖对比:蓝色边缘光勾勒出清晰的几何轮廓,橙色核心光从内部向外辐射,在反射面上投射出动态光影。背景是深邃的黑暗空间,点缀着微小的粒子和数字化光斑。摄影采用低角度透视,35mm镜头,浅景深突出立方体主体,营造出神秘而充满科技感的氛围,具有现代数字科幻美学。

## English

**Glowing Cube**

A glowing, futuristic cube with precise blue luminous grid lines and pulsating orange energy light effects, hovering above a mirror-like reflective surface. The cube's surface presents a translucent holographic material, revealing complex internal geometric structures and flowing data streams. Lighting employs cold-warm contrast: blue edge lights outline crisp geometric contours, while orange core light radiates outward from within, casting dynamic shadows on the reflective plane. The background is a deep dark space, dotted with tiny particles and digitized light spots. Photography uses low-angle perspective, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field highlighting the cube subject, creating a mysterious yet technologically sophisticated atmosphere with modern digital sci-fi aesthetics.
