## 中文

**跨平台相同提示词对比测试**

在过去几周里，我一直在Grok Imagine 1.5 API、Seedance 2.0和Grok Imagine Agent（网页版）之间运行相同提示词的对比测试。研究结果不是哪一个"获胜"。它们在不同维度上失败，对于生产路由来说，失败模式比整体分数更重要。

## English

**Been running same-prompt comparisons across**

Been running same-prompt comparisons across Grok Imagine 1.5 API, Seedance 2.0, and Grok Imagine Agent (web) for the past few weeks. The finding isn't which one "wins". They fail in different dimensions, and the failure modes matter more for production routing than overall scores.
