## 中文

**几何形态的流动变换**

超现实抽象场景。无数发光的几何形体——立方体、球体、棱锥——在黑色虚空中缓慢旋转漂浮。这些形体不断变形：立方体融化成液态再重组为球体，球体分裂成多个小棱锥，棱锥扭曲延展成螺旋带状。每个形体表面都映射着彩虹渐变色彩，随着变形不断流动变化。形体之间有半透明的能量丝线连接，当形体靠近时丝线收紧发出明亮光芒。镜头缓慢旋转环绕整个场景，采用 crane shot 从下方仰视逐渐升起至俯视角度。变形过程流畅自然，如同液体金属的有机运动。背景深黑，仅形体自发光，营造深邃的空间感。抽象艺术风格，数字合成质感，高对比度。

## English

**Flowing Transformation of Geometric Forms**

Surreal abstract scene. Countless luminous geometric forms—cubes, spheres, pyramids—slowly rotating and floating in black void. These forms constantly morph: cubes melt into liquid then reorganize as spheres, spheres split into multiple small pyramids, pyramids twist and extend into spiral ribbons. Each form's surface is mapped with rainbow gradient colors that flow and shift continuously with the transformation. Semi-transparent energy threads connect the forms, tightening and emitting bright light when forms approach each other. Camera slowly rotates around the entire scene, using crane shot from below looking up gradually rising to overhead angle. Transformation process is smooth and natural, like organic movement of liquid metal. Background deep black, only forms self-illuminate, creating profound spatial depth. Abstract art style, digital composite texture, high contrast.
