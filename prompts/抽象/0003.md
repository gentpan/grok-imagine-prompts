## 中文

**蒸汽魔法**

A steaming cup of cappuccino on a polished golden table, where the rising steam transforms into a magical swirl of blooming sunflowers, daisies, and plump raspberries, interwoven with glowing golden particles that shimmer like tiny stars. The latte art in the foam subtly forms a heart shape, evoking warmth and whimsy.

## English

**Steaming Magic**

A steaming cup of cappuccino on a polished golden table, where the rising steam transforms into a magical swirl of blooming sunflowers, daisies, and plump raspberries, interwoven with glowing golden particles that shimmer like tiny stars. The latte art in the foam subtly forms a heart shape, evoking warmth and whimsy.
