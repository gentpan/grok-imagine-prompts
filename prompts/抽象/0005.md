## 中文

**动感姿态**

一位时尚女性,拥有丰盈的头发,以动态姿势坐在复古风格室内的木凳上。阳光透过薄纱窗帘洒进,在图案壁纸和地毯上投下戏剧性的阴影。她身穿现代白色服装,散发着优雅和自信。电影级温暖色调和高对比度照明。

## English

**Dynamic Pose**

A stylish woman with voluminous hair, captured in a dynamic pose while sitting on a wooden stool in a retro-inspired interior. The sunlight streams through sheer curtains, casting dramatic shadows on the patterned wallpaper and carpet. She is dressed in a modern white outfit, exuding elegance and confidence. Cinematic warm tones and high-contrast lighting.
