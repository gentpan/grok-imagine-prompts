## 中文

**霓虹之心**

这是一个美丽的心形,在深邃的黑色背景上脉动着生命。极致强烈的霓虹色彩转换循环——从电光蓝到炽热洋红,从霓虹绿到紫罗兰光晕,色彩流动如液体光波,边缘带有炽烈的光晕效果。心形表面具有光滑的玻璃质感和镜面反射,内部充满流动的能量粒子。必须在心形中央或周围以发光的霓虹字体显示"GROK"字样。整体氛围充满未来感和赛博朋克美学,灯光营造出梦幻而充满活力的视觉冲击。

## English

**Neon Heart**

The image is a beautiful heart, pulsating with life against a deep black background. Extreme intense neon color transformations loop—from electric blue to blazing magenta, from neon green to violet halos, colors flowing like liquid light waves with searing glow effects around the edges. The heart's surface features smooth glass-like texture and mirror reflections, filled internally with flowing energy particles. It must display "GROK" in glowing neon typography at the center or around the heart. The overall atmosphere is filled with futuristic and cyberpunk aesthetics, with lighting creating a dreamlike yet vibrant visual impact.
