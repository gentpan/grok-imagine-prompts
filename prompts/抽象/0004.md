## 中文

**手部解剖蓝图**

一张精美复古的手部解剖学蓝图，用古典英文书写在泛黄的羊皮纸上。画面采用达芬奇风格的解剖绘图手法，详细标注了手部的骨骼结构、肌腱、血管和神经系统。蓝图线条采用深褐色墨水绘制，带有手工绘制的质感和细微的笔触变化。周围环绕着优雅的手写注释和测量数据，使用古体英文字母。羊皮纸边缘略有磨损和污渍，增添历史感。光线柔和，从左上角照射，在纸张上形成微妙的阴影和高光，突显纸张的纹理质感。画面呈现出温暖的棕褐色调，带有文艺复兴时期科学手稿的优雅氛围，充满艺术性和学术性。

## English

**Hand Blueprints**

An exquisite vintage anatomical blueprint of human hands, written in classical English on aged parchment paper. Composition employs Da Vinci-style anatomical drawing technique, meticulously labeling bone structures, tendons, blood vessels, and nervous systems of the hand. Blueprint lines rendered in deep brown ink with handcrafted texture and subtle stroke variations. Surrounded by elegant handwritten annotations and measurement data using archaic English lettering. Parchment edges show slight wear and staining, adding historical authenticity. Soft lighting from upper left corner casting subtle shadows and highlights, emphasizing paper texture quality. Warm sepia-brown color palette conveying Renaissance scientific manuscript elegance, filled with artistic and academic sophistication. Detailed illustration with architectural precision and anatomical accuracy in documentary style.
