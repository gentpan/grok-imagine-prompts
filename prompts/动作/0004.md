## 中文

**电影感 Angles**

超真实电影级镜头, 多个动态摄像机角度, 横扫式环绕摇镜, 缓慢推轨特写, 宽幅建立镜头. 采用电影级2.39:1宽银幕比例，使用多机位拍摄策略捕捉场景的每个维度。开场用广角建立镜头展现环境全貌，配合自然的环境光和柔和的体积雾效。360度环绕镜头以流畅的轨道运动围绕主体旋转，展现空间深度。缓慢推轨镜头从中景逐渐推进到紧密特写，浅景深将背景柔化为梦幻散景。每个角度都经过精心构图，运用三分法则和引导线。照明采用三点布光系统，主光、补光和轮廓光完美平衡，质感丰富而真实。

## English

**Cinematic Angles**

Ultra realistic cinematic footage, multiple dynamic camera angles, sweeping orbital pan, slow dolly-in close-up, wide establishing shot. Employing cinematic 2.39:1 widescreen aspect ratio with multi-camera shooting strategy capturing every dimension of the scene. Opening wide-angle establishing shot reveals full environmental context, paired with natural ambient lighting and soft volumetric haze. 360-degree orbital shot circles subject with fluid track motion, showcasing spatial depth. Slow dolly-in transitions from medium shot to intimate close-up, shallow depth of field softening background into dreamy bokeh. Each angle meticulously composed using rule of thirds and leading lines. Lighting utilizes three-point system with key, fill, and rim lights perfectly balanced, textures rendered rich and authentic.
