## 中文

**Orbit God Rays**

360度环绕镜头，体积照明，上帝射线，zoom-out，视差效果，发光效果，交叉淡入淡出，高分辨率输出，原生音频生成，超真实电影风格。摄像机以流畅的圆周轨道围绕中心主体旋转，同时缓慢拉远展现更广阔的场景。强烈的定向光源从背景穿透大气层，形成壮观的上帝射线（丁达尔效应），体积照明通过空气中的微粒和薄雾可见。镜头运动产生精美的视差效果，前景、中景和背景以不同速度移动，增强空间深度感。发光效果环绕主体边缘形成柔和光晕，光源过曝区域自然绽放。场景转换采用平滑交叉淡入淡出，营造史诗般的电影氛围。

## English

**Orbit God Rays**

360 orbit shot, volumetric lighting, god rays, zoom-out, parallax effect, glow effect, crossfade, high-resolution output, native audio generation, ultra-realistic cinematic style. Camera rotates in smooth circular orbit around central subject while gradually pulling back to reveal broader scene. Intense directional light source penetrates atmosphere from background, forming spectacular god rays (Tyndall effect), volumetric lighting visible through airborne particles and thin mist. Lens movement creates exquisite parallax effect with foreground, midground, and background shifting at different speeds, enhancing spatial depth perception. Glow effect forms soft halo around subject edges, light source bloom naturally in overexposed areas. Scene transitions employ smooth crossfade, establishing epic cinematic atmosphere.
