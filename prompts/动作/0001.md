## English

**Intense Whip Pan**

Handheld micro-shake, whip pan, zoom-in, motion blur, hard lighting, particle effects, physics simulation, fast motion, cuts to, chromatic aberration. Scene illuminated by intense directional hard lighting casting sharp shadows and high contrast. Camera employs rapid whip pan technique, violently cutting from one action subject to another, creating pronounced motion blur and chromatic aberration effects. Handheld shooting style introduces natural micro-shake, enhancing tense atmosphere. Particle systems simulate dust, debris, or sparks flying through the air, paired with physics engine ensuring realistic dynamic interactions. Zoom lens rapidly pushes from wide angle to close-up, emphasizing the impact and velocity of the action.
