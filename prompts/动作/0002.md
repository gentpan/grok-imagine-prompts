## 中文

**Timelapse Lights**

添加 "延时摄影 城市灯光" 到你的提示词中 来创建令人惊叹的 延时摄影 video. 它加速运动 以显示时间的流逝. 从黄昏到深夜，城市灯光从温暖的金色日落过渡到充满活力的霓虹蓝和紫色。车流的灯光在街道上划出流畅的光轨，建筑物窗户逐一亮起。摄像机采用固定三脚架设置，使用广角镜头捕捉城市天际线全景。延时技术将数小时压缩为数秒，展现云层快速移动、星轨旋转、城市节奏的韵律感。自然光与人造光的动态平衡创造出梦幻般的都市氛围，色温从暖色调平滑过渡到冷色调。

## English

**Timelapse Lights**

Add "timelapse city lights" to your prompt to create a stunning timelapse video. It speeds up motion to show the passage of time. From dusk to deep night, city lights transition from warm golden sunset hues to vibrant neon blues and purples. Vehicle lights trace smooth light trails across streets, building windows illuminate one by one. Camera uses fixed tripod setup with wide-angle lens capturing panoramic city skyline. Timelapse technique compresses hours into seconds, showcasing rapidly moving clouds, rotating star trails, and the rhythmic pulse of urban life. Dynamic balance between natural and artificial light creates a dreamlike metropolitan atmosphere, with color temperature smoothly transitioning from warm to cool tones.
