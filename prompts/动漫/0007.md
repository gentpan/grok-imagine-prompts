## 中文

**Tested Grok Imagine 1.5 vs**

在动漫动作提示词上测试了Grok Imagine 1.5与Seedance 2.0。Seedance比当前的炒作周期所暗示的更被低估。测试聚焦于动漫风格的动作场景生成，包括复杂的打斗编排、快速镜头运动、动态特效和夸张的动作表现。评估维度涵盖：动作流畅度、动画帧间过渡的连贯性、角色动态姿势的准确性、特效的视觉冲击力、以及对传统动漫美学的还原度。Seedance 2.0在动作场景的节奏把控、速度线运用、冲击帧处理等动漫特有技法上展现出超出预期的表现力。两个模型在照明风格、色彩饱和度、线条清晰度等方面各有特点，但Seedance在保持动漫风格纯粹性方面更胜一筹。

## English

**Tested Grok Imagine 1.5 vs**

Tested Grok Imagine 1.5 vs Seedance 2.0 on anime action prompts. Seedance is more under-rated than the current hype cycle suggests. Testing focused on anime-style action scene generation, including complex fight choreography, rapid camera movements, dynamic effects, and exaggerated action expressions. Evaluation dimensions encompass: motion fluidity, inter-frame transition coherence, character dynamic pose accuracy, visual impact of effects, and fidelity to traditional anime aesthetics. Seedance 2.0 demonstrates beyond-expected expressiveness in anime-specific techniques like action scene rhythm control, speed line application, and impact frame handling. Both models have distinct characteristics in lighting style, color saturation, and line clarity, but Seedance excels in maintaining anime style purity.
