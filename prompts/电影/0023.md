## 中文

**废弃工厂的觉醒时刻**

一个年轻女子在废弃工厂地面上苏醒，混凝土地面覆满灰尘与裂痕。摄像机从她面部特写开始，然后同时执行 pan up 和 dolly out 的复合运动：镜头向上平移的同时向外推移，逐渐展现整个工厂内部空间——锈蚀的钢梁纵横交错，破碎的天窗漏进柔和的清晨光线，营造出梦幻般的尘埃粒子效果。女子身着破损的白衬衫与牛仔裤，缓缓坐起身，迷茫地环顾四周。镜头继续执行 tilt + dolly 组合运动：向上倾斜同时移动轨道，最终定格在工厂巨大的镂空天花板，鸽子惊飞而起。电影感氛围照明，35mm 胶片质感，浅景深，柔和色调分级。

## English

**Awakening in the Abandoned Factory**

A young woman wakes up on the floor of an abandoned factory, the concrete surface covered with dust and cracks. The camera starts with a close-up of her face, then executes a compound movement of pan up and dolly out simultaneously: the lens pans upward while pulling back, gradually revealing the entire factory interior—rusted steel beams crisscross overhead, broken skylights let in soft morning light, creating a dreamlike dust particle effect. The woman wears a torn white shirt and jeans, slowly sitting up, looking around in confusion. The camera continues with tilt + dolly combined movement: tilting upward while tracking, finally settling on the factory's massive hollow ceiling as pigeons take flight. Cinematic atmospheric lighting, 35mm film grain texture, shallow depth of field, muted color grading.
