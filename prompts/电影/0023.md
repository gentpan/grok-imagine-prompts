## 中文

**Grok Imagine 1.5 (API) follows**

Grok Imagine 1.5 (API)遵循复合摄像机方向。"Pan up and out"/"tilt + dolly"实际执行了。其他两个模型无论复合指令如何都给出了固定或部分的摄像机移动。

## English

**Grok Imagine 1.5 (API) follows**

Grok Imagine 1.5 (API) follows compound camera direction. "Pan up and out" / "tilt + dolly" actually executes. Both other models gave a fixed or partial camera move regardless of the compound instruction.
