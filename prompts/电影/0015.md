## 中文

**Falling debris, hair particles, lens**

falling debris(坠落碎片)、头发粒子、镜头光晕(lens flares)等视觉效果未经请求就自动出现在渲染中。这似乎是模型训练时内化的电影先验知识在自动发挥作用——它学习了商业电影和预告片中常见的这些"增强戏剧性"的视觉元素,并认为应该默认添加它们来提升"电影感"。虽然这些效果在动作片或史诗场景中确实有效,但在需要干净、克制视觉的场景中却成为干扰。幸运的是,使用负面提示词"no falling particles, no lens flare, no debris"可以可靠地清理这些不请自来的效果,恢复导演对画面的完全控制。这凸显了理解模型潜在偏好并主动抑制不需要的默认行为的重要性。

## English

**Falling debris, hair particles, lens**

Falling debris, hair particles, lens flares and other visual effects automatically appear in renders without being requested. This seems to be the model's internalized cinematic prior knowledge automatically firing—it learned these "drama-enhancing" visual elements common in commercial films and trailers, believing they should be added by default to boost "cinematic feel." While these effects are indeed effective in action films or epic scenes, they become distractions in scenes requiring clean, restrained visuals. Fortunately, using negative prompt "no falling particles, no lens flare, no debris" reliably cleans up these uninvited effects, restoring complete directorial control over the frame. This highlights the importance of understanding models' latent preferences and proactively suppressing unwanted default behaviors.
