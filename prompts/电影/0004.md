## 中文

**复古咖啡时刻**

一个女孩正在喝咖啡,画面看起来像是用三星SGH-T100早期手机拍摄的照片,带有典型的低分辨率和颗粒感。慢动作捕捉啜饮瞬间,她脸上浮现惊喜的笑容,眼神中闪烁着愉悦的光芒。照明采用柔和的自然光,可能来自窗边,营造温暖而亲密的氛围。复古滤镜增强了怀旧感——略微过曝的高光,轻微褪色的色彩,带有2000年代早期数码成像的特征性色偏(偏暖的黄绿色调)。画面边缘带有轻微的暗角,质感粗糙但真挚,捕捉了那个时代移动摄影的独特美学——不完美但充满情感。镜头角度自然随意,就像朋友间的即兴抓拍。

## English

**Coffee Sip Retro**

A girl sipping a coffee of what looks like a photo made with a Samsung SGH-T100 early phone camera, featuring typical low resolution and graininess. Slow motion captures the sipping moment, her face breaking into a surprised smile, eyes sparkling with delight. Lighting employs soft natural light, possibly from a window, creating a warm and intimate atmosphere. Vintage filter enhances nostalgia—slightly overblown highlights, subtly faded colors, bearing the characteristic color cast of early 2000s digital imaging (warm yellow-green tones). Frame edges feature slight vignetting, texture rough yet genuine, capturing the unique aesthetic of that era's mobile photography—imperfect but emotionally charged. Lens angle naturally casual, like a spontaneous snapshot between friends.
