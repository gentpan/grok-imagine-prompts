## 中文

**空旷教堂的独唱**

一座哥特式古老教堂内部，阳光透过彩色玻璃窗投下斑驳光影。一位身穿素色长裙的年轻女歌手站在教堂中央，双手轻握在胸前，闭眼轻声哼唱。镜头从侧面特写开始，缓慢 crane up 升起，展现教堂高耸的拱顶与空荡荡的长椅排列。她的歌声在空间中回荡，形成自然混响。无对白，只有纯粹的人声与环境音——远处鸟鸣、风吹动窗帘的沙沙声。光线柔和，尘埃粒子在光束中缓缓飘舞。摄影机保持平稳移动，最终定格在仰视角度，展现女歌手渺小的身影与宏伟建筑的对比。电影级照明，暖色调，浅景深，35mm 胶片质感。

## English

**Solo Performance in an Empty Cathedral**

Inside a Gothic ancient cathedral, sunlight filters through stained glass windows casting mottled light and shadow. A young female singer in a plain long dress stands at the cathedral's center, hands gently clasped at her chest, eyes closed, softly humming. Camera starts with a side close-up, slowly executing a crane up, revealing the cathedral's soaring vaulted ceiling and rows of empty pews. Her voice echoes through the space, creating natural reverb. No dialogue, only pure vocals and ambient sound—distant birdsong, the rustle of curtains swaying in the wind. Soft lighting, dust particles slowly drifting through beams of light. Camera maintains smooth movement, finally settling at an upward angle, showing the contrast between the singer's small figure and the grand architecture. Cinematic lighting, warm tones, shallow depth of field, 35mm film texture.
