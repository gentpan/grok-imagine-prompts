## 中文

**Test setup: 5 character action**

测试设置:5个角色动作提示词(战士类型角色,刻意省略场景说明和摄像机语言参数),设计用于揭示每个模型的内在视觉偏好和默认渲染选择。将完全相同的简洁提示词逐字输入每个模型,不添加任何光照、角度、构图或风格修饰符。这种控制变量的方法暴露了模型在缺乏明确指导时的"自主决策"——它们会自动选择什么样的照明方案(自然光vs戏剧性光影)、构图风格(动态动作vs静态肖像)、相机角度(英雄仰角vs平视)以及整体美学倾向(写实vs风格化)。通过比较输出结果的差异,可以清晰识别每个模型的"个性签名"和最适合的应用场景。

## English

**Test setup: 5 character action**

Test setup: 5 character action prompts (warrior-type characters, deliberately omitting scene specifications and camera language parameters), designed to reveal each model's inherent visual preferences and default rendering choices. Feed the exact same concise prompts verbatim into each model, adding no lighting, angle, composition, or style modifiers. This controlled variable approach exposes models' "autonomous decisions" in absence of explicit guidance—what lighting schemes they automatically choose (natural light vs dramatic chiaroscuro), composition styles (dynamic action vs static portrait), camera angles (heroic upward angle vs eye-level), and overall aesthetic tendencies (realistic vs stylized). By comparing output differences, one can clearly identify each model's "personality signature" and most suitable application scenarios.
