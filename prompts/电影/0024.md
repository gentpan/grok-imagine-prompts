## 中文

**Seedance 2.0 won't execute compound**

Seedance 2.0无法可靠地执行复合摄像机移动。单一方向("dolly in"/"pan left")有效。复合方向被简化为模型视为主导的任何组件。所有4个测试提示词都是相同的模式。音频强度没有补偿。

## English

**Seedance 2.0 won't execute compound**

Seedance 2.0 won't execute compound camera moves reliably. Single direction ("dolly in" / "pan left") works. Compound directions get reduced to whichever component the model treats as dominant. Same pattern across all 4 test prompts. Audio strength didn't compensate.
