## 中文

**我刚进入Grok**

我刚刚进入Grok Imagine，更多是出于病态的好奇心，想看看他们是否继续把它搞得更糟，是的，他们做到了。我立刻意识到的是，我的场景因为"迷人"这个词而在1%时被立即审核。我不是开玩笑，迷人现在是立即审核的词。但这让我想到，一个Imagine禁用词列表对于不浪费每天12个视频配额真的很有帮助。我不会再使用Imagine了，所以这只是一个想法，也许社区中有人想要处理它来帮助其他人。显然xAI（人类历史上最神秘的组织）不会发布禁用词列表，所以我们只能靠自己，就像所有与xAI相关的事情一样。祝你们这些仍然坚持使用该服务的可怜人好运。我真心希望它能为你们恢复到以前的样子。

**I just went into Grok**

I just went into Grok Imagine, more out of morbid curiosity, to see if they've managed to continue making it worse, and yep, they have. What I realized right away is that my scene was being instantly moderated at 1% because of the word "alluring". I'm not kidding, alluring is now an instant moderation. But that got me thinking, a list of words that imagine has banned would be really helpful for not wasting your 12 videos per day. I won't be using Imagine again, so it's just an idea that maybe somebody in the community would want to tackle to help out everyone else. Obviously xai (the most secretive organization in human history) won't be putting out a list of banned words, so we're on our own, as with all things xai related.
