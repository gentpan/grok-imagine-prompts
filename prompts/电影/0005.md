## 中文

**迷人的午夜爵士酒吧**

深夜的地下爵士酒吧，昏暗迷人的氛围。一位穿着红色丝绸长裙的女歌手站在小舞台上，手持复古麦克风，在柔和的聚光灯下演唱。她的轮廓被暖黄色灯光勾勒，背景中烟雾缭绕，乐队成员若隐若现——萨克斯手、钢琴师、鼓手。台下观众坐在圆桌前，香烟的烟雾在桌灯光束中上升。摄像机采用缓慢的dolly in 推进镜头，从吧台后方开始，穿过观众，逐渐接近舞台上的歌手。色彩分级采用温暖的琥珀色调与深蓝阴影对比，营造1940年代黑色电影氛围。浅景深，焦点在歌手面部，背景与前景自然虚化。35mm胶片质感，颗粒感明显，复古电影美学。

## English

**Alluring Midnight Jazz Bar**

Late night underground jazz bar, dimly alluring atmosphere. A female singer in a red silk dress stands on a small stage, holding a vintage microphone, performing under soft spotlight. Her silhouette outlined by warm yellow light, background filled with swirling smoke, band members barely visible—saxophonist, pianist, drummer. Audience below seated at round tables, cigarette smoke rising through table lamp beams. Camera uses slow dolly in push shot, starting from behind the bar, moving through the audience, gradually approaching the singer on stage. Color grading employs warm amber tones contrasted with deep blue shadows, creating 1940s film noir atmosphere. Shallow depth of field, focus on singer's face, foreground and background naturally blurred. 35mm film texture, prominent grain, vintage cinema aesthetic.
