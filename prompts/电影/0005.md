**I just went into Grok**

I just went into Grok Imagine, more out of morbid curiosity, to see if they've managed to continue making it worse, and yep, they have. What I realized right away is that my scene was being instantly moderated at 1% because of the word "alluring". I'm not kidding, alluring is now an instant moderation. But that got me thinking, a list of words that imagine has banned would be really helpful for not wasting your 12 videos per day. I won't be using Imagine again, so it's just an idea that maybe somebody in the community would want to tackle to help out everyone else. Obviously xai (the most secretive organization in human history) won't be putting out a list of banned words, so we're on our own, as with all things xai related. Good luck to you poor bastards who are still sticking with the service. I truly hope it gets back to the way it was for y'all. submitted by /u/Makai1847 [link] [comments]
