## 中文

**这意味着当你**

这意味着当你想象一个性场景时，你的大脑正在运行与该场景实际发生时相同的处理过程。整个情色价值引擎都在运作，并且它在大脑编造的场景上运作。画面以抽象艺术风格呈现神经网络的视觉化,采用温暖的琥珀色和深紫色渐变照明,营造出梦幻般的意识流动感。镜头采用缓慢推进的dolly in运动,配合柔焦效果和光晕散射,突出大脑思维活动的流动性。纹理表现为半透明的神经突触连接,带有微妙的粒子效果和柔和的辉光,整体氛围神秘而富有诗意。

## English

**This means that when you**

This means that when you imagine a sexual scenario, your brain is running the same kind of processing it would run if the scenario were actually happening. The whole erotic value engine is operational, and it's operational on a scene the brain made up. Presented in abstract artistic style visualizing neural networks with warm amber and deep purple gradient lighting creating dreamlike consciousness flow. Camera employs slow dolly-in movement paired with soft focus and light halo diffusion, emphasizing the fluidity of brain thought activity. Textures rendered as translucent synaptic connections with subtle particle effects and soft glow, overall atmosphere mysterious yet poetic.
