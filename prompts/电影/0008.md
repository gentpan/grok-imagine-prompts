## 中文

**战士在废墟中的决战**

中景镜头。一位身穿破损盔甲的战士站在古代神庙废墟中，双手握持巨剑，剑尖指向地面。战士占画面 40-60%，足以展现全身姿态与肢体语言，同时保持面部表情清晰可读。废墟背景充满情绪化的环境元素：倒塌的石柱、破碎的雕像、蔓延的藤蔓。逆光从战士身后透过破损的屋顶射入，勾勒出轮廓光，营造戏剧性氛围。尘埃粒子在光束中缓缓飘浮。战士目光坚毅，身体微微前倾，随时准备战斗。摄影机采用略低于眼线的角度，强调战士的力量感。电影级动作摄影美学，高对比度照明，暖色调偏移，35mm 镜头焦距。

## English

**Warrior's Showdown in the Ruins**

Mid-shot framing. A warrior in damaged armor stands amid ancient temple ruins, both hands gripping a greatsword with the tip pointing to the ground. The warrior occupies 40-60% of the frame, enough to show full body stance and body language while keeping facial expression clearly readable. The ruined background is filled with moody environmental elements: collapsed stone pillars, broken statues, creeping vines. Rim lighting from behind the warrior shines through the damaged roof, creating a silhouette edge that builds dramatic atmosphere. Dust particles slowly drift through the light beams. The warrior's gaze is resolute, body slightly leaning forward, ready for combat. Camera uses a slightly below eye-level angle to emphasize the warrior's sense of power. Cinematic action photography aesthetic, high-contrast lighting, warm color shift, 35mm focal length.
