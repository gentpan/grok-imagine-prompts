## 中文

**视频运动模板**

让[主体]以[运动风格]执行[动作或行为],使用[摄像机风格],循环动画,真实运动物理,4K分辨率,24fps。动作设计遵循真实的运动曲线和物理惯性——加速、减速和动量转换自然流畅,身体各部位的次要动作(如头发飘动、衣物摆动)与主动作完美同步。照明跟随运动变化——高光和阴影随着角度改变动态移动,反射和折射效果真实呈现。摄像机运动根据动作节奏调整——可以是跟拍、环绕、俯冲或推拉,保持主体在画面中的最佳构图位置。景深和运动模糊模拟真实摄影机的光学特性,快速动作带有自然的方向性模糊。整体氛围营造电影级的视觉质感和叙事张力。

## English

**Video Motion Template**

Make the [subject] perform [motion or action] in a [style of movement], with [camera style], looped animation, realistic motion physics, 4K resolution, 24fps. Motion design follows authentic movement curves and physical inertia—acceleration, deceleration, and momentum transfer naturally fluid, secondary actions of body parts (like hair flowing, clothing swaying) perfectly synchronized with primary motion. Lighting follows motion changes—highlights and shadows dynamically shift with angle changes, reflection and refraction effects realistically rendered. Camera movement adjusts to action rhythm—can be tracking, orbiting, diving, or push-pull, maintaining optimal composition position of subject in frame. Depth of field and motion blur simulate real camera optical characteristics, rapid actions featuring natural directional blur. Overall atmosphere creates cinematic visual texture and narrative tension.
