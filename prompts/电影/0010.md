## 中文

**Subject takes 40-60% of frame.**

主体占据画面的40-60%——这是电影摄影中的黄金构图比例。足够宽以完整展示肢体语言、战斗姿态和武器细节,同时又足够紧凑以清晰读取面部表情和眼神情绪。背景通常选择小巷、废墟或带有轮廓光的氛围环境,为角色提供戏剧性的视觉语境而不喧宾夺主。照明采用三点式布光变体:强烈的侧光或背光勾勒人物轮廓(rim lighting),创造立体感和英雄气质;适度的填充光保留暗部细节;环境光营造整体氛围。景深控制在f/2.8-f/4范围,确保主体锐利、背景柔和虚化。构图呈现经典的电影动作摄影美学——充满张力、视觉冲击力强,同时保持叙事清晰度。

## English

**Subject takes 40-60% of frame.**

Subject takes 40-60% of frame—the golden composition ratio in cinematography. Wide enough to fully display body language, combat stance, and weapon details, yet tight enough to clearly read facial expressions and eye emotions. Background typically features alleys, ruins, or moody environments with rim lighting, providing dramatic visual context for the character without overwhelming the subject. Lighting employs three-point lighting variations: strong side or backlighting sculpts character contours (rim lighting), creating dimensionality and heroic quality; moderate fill light preserves shadow details; ambient light establishes overall atmosphere. Depth of field controlled at f/2.8-f/4 range, ensuring subject sharpness with soft background bokeh. Composition presents classic cinematic action photography aesthetic—tension-filled, visually impactful, while maintaining narrative clarity.
