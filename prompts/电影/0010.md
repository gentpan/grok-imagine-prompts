## 中文

**Subject takes 40-60% of frame.**

主体占据画面的40-60%。足够宽以展示肢体语言和姿态,足够紧以读取面部表情。背景通常是小巷、废墟或带有轮廓光的氛围环境。构图呈现电影动作摄影美学。

## English

**Subject takes 40-60% of frame.**

Subject takes 40-60% of frame. Wide enough to show body language and stance, tight enough to read facial expression. Background is typically alley, ruin, or moody environment with rim lighting. Composition reads as cinematic action photography aesthetic.
