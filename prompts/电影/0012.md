## 中文

**恐怖场景：走廊尽头的门**

阴暗的公寓走廊，墙面斑驳脱落，唯一的光源是尽头门缝透出的微弱黄光。一位年轻女性站在走廊入口，身体微微后倾，恐惧地注视着那扇门。镜头采用低角度 Dutch tilt 构图，营造不安感。突然，门缝的光线闪烁，女性的头部缓慢转向门口，身体却因恐惧而僵硬不动。无对白，只有环境音：远处的水滴声、老旧建筑的嘎吱声、她压抑的呼吸声。硬切到门把手的特写——锈迹斑斑的把手开始缓慢转动。再硬切回女性面部，瞳孔放大。镜头保持静止，无过渡转场，强调恐怖的突然性。暗调照明，高对比度，无镜头光晕，无飘落粒子效果。

## English

**Horror Scene: The Door at the End of the Hallway**

A dim apartment hallway, walls peeling and mottled, the only light source is a faint yellow glow seeping through a doorway at the far end. A young woman stands at the hallway entrance, body leaning slightly back, staring at that door in fear. Camera uses low-angle Dutch tilt composition to create unease. Suddenly, the light from the door flickers, the woman's head slowly turns toward the doorway, but her body remains frozen in fear. No dialogue, ambient audio only: distant water dripping, creaking of an old building, her suppressed breathing. Hard cut to close-up of the doorknob—rusted handle begins to turn slowly. Hard cut back to the woman's face, pupils dilating. Camera remains static, no transitions, emphasizing the abruptness of horror. Low-key lighting, high contrast, no lens flares, no falling particles.
