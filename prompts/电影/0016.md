## 中文

**The pattern: Grok Imagine 1.5**

模式:Grok Imagine 1.5具有强大的电影默认设置,除非明确否定否则会触发。在相同设置下测试的其他模型(特别是Seedance 2.0)需要明确的正面指令来添加这些元素。处于先验与提示词光谱的对立两端。

## English

**The pattern: Grok Imagine 1.5**

The pattern: Grok Imagine 1.5 has strong cinematic defaults that fire unless explicitly negated. Other models tested in the same setup (Seedance 2.0 in particular) require explicit positive instruction to add these elements. Opposite ends of the prior-versus-prompt spectrum.
