## 中文

**雨夜霓虹街头追逐**

深夜的东京街头，大雨倾盆。一名穿黑色风衣的侦探在霓虹灯照耀的街道上狂奔，镜头采用 Steadicam 跟拍，与他保持平行移动。街道两侧的日文招牌反射在湿滑的柏油路面上，形成五彩斑斓的光影。侦探突然转身钻入小巷，镜头执行快速 whip pan 跟随，捕捉他翻越铁栏杆的动作。雨滴在镜头前形成自然的景深效果，远处追赶的脚步声与喘息声混合着雨声。电影级色彩分级，高对比度，Anamorphic 镜头光晕，35mm 胶片颗粒感，2.39:1 宽银幕构图。

## English

**Neon Street Chase in the Rain**

Late night in Tokyo streets, heavy rain pouring. A detective in a black trench coat sprints through neon-lit streets, camera using Steadicam tracking to maintain parallel movement with him. Japanese signs on both sides of the street reflect on the wet asphalt, creating colorful light patterns. The detective suddenly turns into an alley, camera executes a rapid whip pan to follow, capturing his action vaulting over an iron railing. Raindrops create natural depth-of-field effects in front of the lens, with distant footsteps and breathing sounds mixing with the rain. Cinematic color grading, high contrast, anamorphic lens flares, 35mm film grain, 2.39:1 widescreen composition.
