## 中文

**Imagined scenes recruit the same**

想象的场景调用与看到的场景相同的对象代码(Wadia等人,2025)。即使回忆痛苦的经历也会激活参与实际疼痛处理的相同情感和情绪大脑区域(Shimo等人,2011)。

## English

**Imagined scenes recruit the same**

Imagined scenes recruit the same object code as seen scenes (Wadia et al., 2025). Even recalling a painful experience activates the same affective and emotional brain regions involved in actual pain processing (Shimo et al., 2011).
