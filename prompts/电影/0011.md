## 中文

**A Grok shot followed by**

一个Grok镜头followed by a Seedance镜头读起来就像中景突然切到极端特写。即使角色设计完全相同,观众的眼睛也会将镜头大小的跳跃解读为导演的选择(实际并非如此)。

## English

**A Grok shot followed by**

A Grok shot followed by a Seedance shot reads as a mid-shot suddenly cutting to extreme close-up. Even with identical character design, the viewer's eye reads the shot-size jump as a director choice (which it isn't).
