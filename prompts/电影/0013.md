## 中文

**阴森地下室的探索**

一位年轻女性手持摇曳的油灯，缓慢走下腐朽的木楼梯进入黑暗地下室。镜头采用 Steadicam 跟随拍摄，与她保持相同速度下降，油灯光线在石墙上投射出跳动的影子。每一步都伴随木板的嘎吱声，尘埃在光束中飘舞。地下室深处隐约可见陈旧的家具轮廓与蜘蛛网。女性停下脚步，举起油灯照向远处黑暗角落，光线缓慢扫过空间，展现被遗忘的物品——破损的娃娃、生锈的工具、覆满霉菌的书籍。她的呼吸声与油灯火焰的嘶嘶声成为唯一的声音。无对白，环境音主导。低调照明，只有油灯作为光源，高对比度，深黑阴影。恐怖片氛围，强调不安感与未知恐惧。

## English

**Exploration of a Sinister Basement**

A young woman holding a flickering oil lamp slowly descends decaying wooden stairs into a dark basement. Camera uses Steadicam tracking, descending at the same pace, the lamp's light casting dancing shadows on stone walls. Each step accompanied by the creak of wooden planks, dust drifting through light beams. Deep in the basement, vague silhouettes of old furniture and cobwebs are visible. The woman pauses, raising the lamp to illuminate a distant dark corner, light slowly sweeping across the space, revealing forgotten items—broken dolls, rusty tools, mold-covered books. Her breathing and the hiss of the lamp's flame become the only sounds. No dialogue, ambient audio dominant. Low-key lighting with only the oil lamp as light source, high contrast, deep black shadows. Horror film atmosphere, emphasizing unease and unknown fear.
