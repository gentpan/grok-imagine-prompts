## 中文

**Been running horror scenes through**

一直在通过Grok Imagine 1.5预览API运行恐怖场景测试。图像质量本身很扎实——渲染清晰,光照真实,细节丰富。问题不在于技术质量,而在于模型在未被明确要求的情况下自动添加的unprompted行为和视觉元素。这些"自作主张"的添加包括:意外的镜头运动(如推进或晃动效果)、未请求的特效(粒子、光斑、雾气)、改变的色彩分级(过度饱和或去饱和)、以及修改的构图选择。这些干预虽然在某些情境下可能增强氛围,但在精确的导演控制中却成为障碍——尤其在需要保持特定视觉一致性或遵循严格故事板的恐怖片制作中。理解并抑制这些默认行为对于专业应用至关重要。

## English

**Been running horror scenes through**

Been running horror scene tests through Grok Imagine 1.5 preview API. Image quality itself is solid—crisp rendering, realistic lighting, rich details. The issue isn't technical quality, it's unprompted behaviors and visual elements the model automatically adds without explicit request. These "self-initiated" additions include: unexpected camera movements (like push-in or shake effects), unrequested special effects (particles, lens flares, fog), altered color grading (over-saturation or desaturation), and modified composition choices. While these interventions might enhance atmosphere in some contexts, they become obstacles in precise directorial control—especially in horror film production requiring specific visual consistency or strict storyboard adherence. Understanding and suppressing these default behaviors is crucial for professional applications.
