## 中文

**The body’s job in all**

身体在这一切中的作用是下游的。心跳加速、皮肤潮红和生殖器反应是在大脑构建场景并产生欲望之后发生的。它们是自主系统遵循大脑指令，就像恐惧中心跳加速是身体遵循杏仁核指令一样。

## English

**The body’s job in all**

The body’s job in all of this is downstream. The pounding heart, flushed skin, and genital response are what happens after the brain has constructed the scene and generated the desire. They’re the autonomic system following the brain’s instructions, the same way the racing heart in fear is the body following the amygdala’s instructions.
