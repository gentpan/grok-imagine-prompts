## 中文

**赛博朋克雨夜小巷**

深夜的城市小巷，大雨倾盆而下，霓虹灯光在湿滑的地面上形成绚丽的彩色倒影。镜头采用手持拍摄风格，带有轻微的晃动和呼吸感，营造出纪实般的临场感。使用35mm胶片镜头拍摄，具有经典的电影质感和适度的颗粒感。色调以青色、品红色和紫色霓虹光为主，冷暖色调对比强烈。雨滴在镜头前形成浅景深的虚化光斑，增强氛围感。小巷两侧是老旧的砖墙，悬挂着日文和中文的霓虹招牌，散发着赛博朋克美学。远处人影模糊，为场景增添神秘感。整体呈现出《银翼杀手》式的黑色电影风格，充满未来主义的颓废美感。

## English

**Cyberpunk Rainy Alley**

A deep-night urban alleyway with heavy rain pouring down, vibrant neon lights creating brilliant colorful reflections on slick wet pavement. Handheld camera style with subtle shaking and breathing motion, creating documentary-like immersive presence. Shot with 35mm film lens featuring classic cinematic texture and moderate film grain. Color palette dominated by cyan, magenta, and purple neon glow with strong cool-warm contrast. Raindrops forming shallow depth-of-field bokeh light spots in foreground, enhancing atmospheric mood. Weathered brick walls flanking the alley, adorned with Japanese and Chinese neon signs emanating cyberpunk aesthetic. Blurred silhouettes in the distance adding mysterious ambiance. Overall composition presenting Blade Runner-style film noir aesthetic, filled with futuristic dystopian beauty and cinematic depth.
