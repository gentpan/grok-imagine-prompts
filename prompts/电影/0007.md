## 中文

**Wazir fixes this. You describe**

Wazir解决了这个问题。你用简单的英语描述你的场景——无需掌握复杂的技术术语或特定的提示词语法。你选择你的目标模型(Grok、Seedance、Kling或其他)。系统会生成该模型所需的精确提示词——基于3个月深入的实际生产研究和数千次迭代测试构建,而非通用的AI输出或模糊的猜测。每个模型对照明术语、相机语言、构图描述和风格修饰符的响应方式都截然不同。Wazir的提示词转换引擎理解这些细微差异,自动调整参数权重、关键词顺序和修饰符组合,确保在不同模型间获得一致且专业的视觉效果,大幅提升创作效率和输出质量。

## English

**Wazir fixes this. You describe**

Wazir fixes this. You describe your scene in plain English—no need to master complex technical terminology or specific prompt syntax. You pick your target model (Grok, Seedance, Kling, or others). The system generates the exact prompt that model needs—built from 3 months of intensive real production research and thousands of iterative tests, not generic AI output or vague guessing. Each model responds to lighting terminology, camera language, composition descriptions, and style modifiers in drastically different ways. Wazir's prompt translation engine understands these nuances, automatically adjusting parameter weights, keyword order, and modifier combinations, ensuring consistent and professional visual results across different models, dramatically improving creative efficiency and output quality.
