## 中文

**图像生成模板**

高度细致的[主体],呈现[风格或时代]风格,场景设定在[场景或背景],采用电影级构图,柔和光影,真实质感,35mm镜头景深,ArtStation热门,精致入微,超写实主义。照明设置结合自然光与人工光源——柔和的侧光勾勒轮廓,暖色调的填充光营造氛围,背光创造层次感。相机运动流畅而克制,采用轻微的推轨或稳定器拍摄,焦点精准锁定主体,背景呈现梦幻般的散景效果。材质表现注重细节——皮肤的微妙纹理、织物的自然褶皱、金属的真实反射,每个表面都具有物理准确的光照响应。整体色调经过精心分级,呈现电影级的色彩深度和对比度,氛围既真实又充满艺术感染力。

## English

**Image Gen Template**

A highly detailed [subject] in a [style or era] style, set in a [scene or background], captured using a cinematic composition, soft lighting, realistic textures, 35mm lens depth of field, trending on ArtStation, intricate, hyperrealism. Lighting setup combines natural and artificial light sources—soft side lighting sculpts contours, warm-toned fill light creates atmosphere, backlighting establishes depth. Camera movement is smooth and restrained, employing subtle dolly or stabilizer shots, focus precisely locked on subject, background presenting dreamlike bokeh effects. Material representation emphasizes detail—subtle skin textures, natural fabric folds, authentic metal reflections, each surface exhibiting physically accurate lighting response. Overall color tone is carefully graded, presenting cinematic color depth and contrast, atmosphere both realistic and artistically compelling.
