## English

**Prompt doesn't mention transitions. Grok**

Prompt doesn't mention transitions. Grok inserts them anyway between consecutive shots. Reads like the model defaults to "transition exists" instead of "no transition specified means hard cut." Breaks rhythm on horror scenes where you want abrupt cuts.
