## 中文

**Prompt doesn't mention transitions. Grok**

提示词明确没有提到过渡效果。但Grok还是在连续镜头之间自动插入了它们——淡入淡出、溶解、模糊过渡等。看起来模型的内部逻辑默认为"过渡应该存在"而不是遵循电影剪辑的标准约定"未指定过渡意味着硬切(hard cut)"。这在需要突然、震撼性切换的恐怖场景中严重破坏了节奏和情绪张力。恐怖片导演经常依赖硬切来制造惊吓效果——从平静的镜头瞬间切到威胁画面,不给观众准备时间。柔和的过渡削弱了这种冲击力,让吓人的时刻变得可预测。这种"过度优雅"的默认行为需要通过负面提示或明确指令来抑制,才能实现导演意图的视觉节奏。

## English

**Prompt doesn't mention transitions. Grok**

Prompt explicitly doesn't mention transition effects. Yet Grok automatically inserts them between consecutive shots—fades, dissolves, blur transitions, etc. Reads like the model's internal logic defaults to "transitions should exist" rather than following the standard convention in film editing "no transition specified means hard cut." This severely disrupts rhythm and emotional tension in horror scenes requiring sudden, shocking cuts. Horror directors frequently rely on hard cuts to create scare effects—instantly cutting from calm shots to threatening imagery, giving the audience no preparation time. Soft transitions weaken this impact, making frightening moments predictable. This "over-elegant" default behavior needs suppression through negative prompts or explicit instructions to achieve the director's intended visual rhythm.
