## 中文

**模糊焦点**

摄像机聚焦于背景中的老师，前景中的女孩变得模糊，老师非常愤怒地咒骂。（多重动作，非固定镜头，5秒）

## English

**Blurred Focus**

The camera focuses on the teacher in the background, the girl in the foreground becomes blurred, and the teacher curses very angrily. (Multiple actions, unfixed lens, 5s).
