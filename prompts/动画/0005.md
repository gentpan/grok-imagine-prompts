## 中文

**模糊焦点**

摄像机聚焦于背景中的老师，前景中的女孩变得模糊，老师非常愤怒地咒骂。（多重动作，非固定镜头，5秒）。采用拉焦技术（rack focus），镜头焦点从前景的失焦女孩背影平滑过渡到背景中的老师脸部，景深效果明显。前景女孩呈现柔美散景效果，轮廓模糊成色块。老师表情激动，面部肌肉紧绷，手势幅度夸张，展现强烈的情绪爆发。照明为教室内的自然窗光混合顶部荧光灯，色温偏冷白。镜头略带手持晃动感，增强现场真实性和紧张氛围。画面构图采用景深层次，展现空间关系和人物互动的戏剧张力。音频中能听到老师提高的嗓音和愤怒的语气。

## English

**Blurred Focus**

The camera focuses on the teacher in the background, the girl in the foreground becomes blurred, and the teacher curses very angrily. (Multiple actions, unfixed lens, 5s). Employing rack focus technique, lens focal point smoothly transitions from out-of-focus girl silhouette in foreground to teacher's face in background, depth of field effect pronounced. Foreground girl presents beautiful bokeh effect, contours blurred into color patches. Teacher's expression agitated, facial muscles tense, gestures exaggerated, showcasing intense emotional outburst. Lighting consists of classroom natural window light mixed with overhead fluorescent tubes, color temperature leaning cool white. Camera carries slight handheld shake, enhancing on-scene authenticity and tense atmosphere. Frame composition utilizes depth layering, revealing spatial relationships and dramatic tension of character interaction. Audio features teacher's raised voice and angry tone.
