## 中文

**Visually, the design leverages scale**

Visually, the design leverages scale to great effect. The towering figure casts long shadows over alleyways and market squares, turning ordinary streets into dramatic stages for pursuit and evasion. The environment responds to this scale with dynamic lighting, procedural crowds, and subtle audio cues that signal the guardian’s presence. Players feel the weight of each step, the tremor of the city as it anticipates the next move in a game where every choice matters.
