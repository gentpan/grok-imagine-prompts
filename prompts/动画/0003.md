## 中文

**兔子蹦跳**

夜间监控摄像头拍摄的黑白红外录像，记录了一群兔子在后院蹦床上跳跃的有趣场景。画面采用固定机位，略带鱼眼镜头畸变效果，右下角显示时间戳"23:47:32"。红外照明下，兔子呈现出标志性的夜视效果白色轮廓，眼睛反射出微弱的光点。三只兔子轮流在蹦床上蹦跳，捕捉到它们跳跃的滞空瞬间和落地时蹦床的回弹动态。背景是模糊的后院轮廓，栅栏和灌木丛在夜色中若隐若现。画面带有轻微的噪点和扫描线，增强真实监控录像的质感。整体氛围既诙谐又略带神秘。

## English

**Bunny Bounce**

Black-and-white infrared security camera footage capturing an amusing scene of rabbits jumping on a backyard trampoline at night. Fixed camera position with slight fisheye lens distortion effect, timestamp "23:47:32" displayed in bottom right corner. Under infrared illumination, rabbits appear in characteristic night-vision white silhouettes with faint reflective eye glints. Three rabbits taking turns bouncing on the trampoline, capturing their airborne suspension moments and the trampoline's dynamic rebound upon landing. Blurred backyard silhouettes in background with fence and shrubs barely visible in darkness. Footage features subtle grain noise and scan lines enhancing authentic surveillance recording texture. Overall atmosphere both whimsical and slightly mysterious with documentary-style realism.
