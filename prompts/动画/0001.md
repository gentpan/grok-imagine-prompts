## 中文

**Heart Loop**

这是一个美丽的心形。极致强烈的霓虹色彩转换循环。必须显示"GROK"。女性旁白说："Grok Imagine，创意无限的地方"。心形以饱和的霓虹色彩持续脉动变换，从电蓝到洋红、从翠绿到金黄，色彩过渡流畅如液体流动。发光的"GROK"字样嵌入心形中央，带有柔和的外发光效果。背景为深邃的黑色或深紫色，强化霓虹效果的冲击力。动画完美循环，无缝衔接首尾。照明效果模拟真实霓虹管的辉光特性，边缘带有细腻的光晕扩散。温暖而充满活力的女性旁白配音与视觉节奏完美同步。整体呈现80年代复古未来主义美学。

## English

**Heart Loop**

The image is a beautiful heart. Extreme intense neon color transformations loop. It must say "GROK". The female narrator says "Grok Imagine, where creativity is limitless". Heart shape continuously pulsates with saturated neon colors transforming from electric blue to magenta, from emerald green to golden yellow, color transitions flowing smoothly like liquid. Glowing "GROK" lettering embedded at heart center with soft outer glow effect. Background in deep black or dark purple, intensifying neon impact. Animation loops perfectly with seamless beginning-to-end connection. Lighting effects simulate authentic neon tube characteristics with delicate halo diffusion at edges. Warm and vibrant female voiceover narration synchronizes perfectly with visual rhythm. Overall presenting 80s retrofuturism aesthetic.
