## 中文

**Pet Feast Switch**

小猫和小狗吃猫粮。镜头切换。特写猫粮清晰可辨。(镜头切换,非固定镜头,5秒)。开场镜头以中景展现小猫和小狗并排进食的温馨场景，柔和的自然光从侧面照射，在它们毛发上形成细腻的质感光晕。随后快速剪辑切换到食碗的微距特写，猫粮颗粒的形状、色泽、表面纹理清晰可见，浅景深将背景中模糊的宠物轮廓衬托出主体。能看到小动物的爪子进入画面，抓取食物的动作生动自然。照明强调食物的食欲感，暖色调营造温馨居家氛围。镜头间通过跳切连接，节奏明快。可以听到咀嚼声、碗碰撞声等环境音效，增强真实感和可爱氛围。

## English

**Pet Feast Switch**

Kittens and puppies eat cat food. Shot Switch. Close-up cat food is distinct. (Shot switch, unfixed lens, 5s). Opening shot presents medium view of heartwarming scene with kitten and puppy eating side by side, soft natural light illuminating from the side, forming delicate texture halo on their fur. Then rapidly cuts to macro close-up of food bowl, cat kibble granules' shape, color, surface texture clearly visible, shallow depth of field setting blurred pet silhouettes in background to contrast subject. Small animal paws entering frame, grabbing food with vivid natural motion. Lighting emphasizes food's appetizing quality, warm color palette creating cozy domestic atmosphere. Shots connected through jump cuts, rhythm brisk. Audio features chewing sounds, bowl clinking and other ambient effects, enhancing authenticity and adorable atmosphere.
