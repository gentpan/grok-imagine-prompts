## 中文

**机械蜂鸟的花园之旅**

一只精致的机械蜂鸟在盛开的花园中飞舞，翅膀由透明的齿轮与发条构成，每次扇动都能看到内部精密的机械运转。蜂鸟从一朵巨大的红色玫瑰飞向紫色薰衣草丛，镜头采用流畅的跟随拍摄，保持与蜂鸟的相对位置。花朵随微风轻轻摇曳，花粉粒子在空中闪烁。蜂鸟在薰衣草上方悬停，机械喙部伸出吸取花蜜，身体微微上下振动保持悬浮。背景是柔焦的花园景色，暖色调阳光透过树叶洒落。3D 动画风格，流畅的 60fps 动作，细腻的机械质感与自然元素对比。电影级照明，景深效果突出主体。

## English

**Mechanical Hummingbird's Garden Journey**

A delicate mechanical hummingbird dances through a blooming garden, its wings made of transparent gears and clockwork, each flap revealing intricate internal mechanical movements. The hummingbird flies from a large red rose to purple lavender bushes, camera using smooth tracking to maintain relative position with the bird. Flowers sway gently in the breeze, pollen particles sparkling in the air. The hummingbird hovers above the lavender, mechanical beak extending to extract nectar, body slightly bobbing up and down to maintain suspension. Background is a soft-focus garden scene, warm sunlight filtering through leaves. 3D animation style, smooth 60fps motion, delicate mechanical texture contrasting with natural elements. Cinematic lighting, depth of field effect highlighting the subject.
