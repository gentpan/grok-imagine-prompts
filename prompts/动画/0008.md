## 中文

**AI City Navigate**

一个超风格化的动画,展示一个完全由AI生成的未来主义城市,其中每栋建筑、车辆和市民都由AI设计,具有逼真的纹理与异想天开、超现实的元素混合。场景跟随一个单独的角色,可能是人类或机器人,在黄昏时穿梭于繁华的街道,头顶全息广告闪烁,飞行无人机在高耸的尖塔间穿梭。

## English

**AI City Navigate**

A hyper-stylized animation of a fully AI-generated futuristic city where every building, vehicle, and citizen is designed by AI, featuring realistic textures mixed with whimsical, surreal elements. The scene follows a single character, either human or android, navigating bustling streets at dusk, with holographic ads flickering overhead and flying drones zipping between towering spires.
