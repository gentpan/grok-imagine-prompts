**Prompting local models is far**

根据我的经验,本地模型的提示词要比Grok困难得多。特别是像摄像机移动这样的事情,让人做任何动态的事情都要困难得多。

**Prompting local models is far**

Prompting local models is far more difficult to get right than Grok, in my experience. Especially things like camera moves, getting people to do anything dynamic has been far more difficult.
