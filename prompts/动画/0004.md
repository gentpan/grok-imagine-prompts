## 中文

**Glasses Elder**

老人戴着眼镜。(图像转视频,来自留胡子的男人肖像,固定镜头,10秒时长)。这是一个从静态肖像照片转换而来的微动画视频。老人拥有浓密的花白胡须和深邃的眼神，透过眼镜镜片观察世界。固定镜头保持画面稳定，捕捉细微的自然动作：眼睛的眨动、呼吸引起的胸部轻微起伏、胡须随呼吸轻柔飘动。照明采用柔和的伦勃朗式布光，一侧脸部受到主光源照明，在鼻子另一侧形成经典的三角形高光。眼镜片上有细腻的反光，展现真实的光学特性。皮肤纹理、皱纹、毛孔清晰可见，呈现岁月的痕迹。背景略微虚化，营造深度感。整体氛围沉静、睿智，富有人文情感。

## English

**Glasses Elder**

The old man wears glasses. (Image-to-video from bearded man portrait, fixed lens, 10s duration). This is a subtle animation video converted from a static portrait photograph. The elderly man possesses thick salt-and-pepper beard and profound gaze, observing the world through eyeglass lenses. Fixed camera maintains stable frame, capturing subtle natural movements: eye blinking, gentle chest rise from breathing, beard softly swaying with breath. Lighting employs soft Rembrandt-style setup, one side of face illuminated by key light, forming classic triangular highlight on opposite side of nose. Eyeglass lenses feature delicate reflections showcasing authentic optical properties. Skin texture, wrinkles, pores clearly visible, presenting marks of time. Background slightly defocused, creating sense of depth. Overall atmosphere tranquil, wise, rich with humanistic emotion.
