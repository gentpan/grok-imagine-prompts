## 中文

**推轨定格**

专业电影运镜技巧：镜头平稳缓慢地向前推进，使用轨道推车（dolly）从中景逐渐推进至中景特写景别。运动速度控制均匀柔和，营造出渐进的情感张力。当到达预定的中景特写构图位置后，镜头完全静止，保持1秒钟的定格画面，让观众充分感受画面细节和情绪氛围。焦点跟随主体保持锐利清晰，背景产生轻微的景深变化。这个停顿时刻在剪辑前起到强调作用，为下一个镜头切换做好情绪铺垫。专业摄影机运动，电影级画面稳定性，流畅的运镜节奏。

## English

**Dolly Hold**

Professional cinematic camera movement technique: camera smoothly and slowly tracks forward using a dolly track system, progressing from medium shot to medium close-up framing. Movement speed controlled evenly and gently, building gradual emotional tension. Upon reaching the predetermined medium close-up composition position, camera comes to complete stillness, holding the frame for 1 second, allowing viewers to fully absorb visual details and emotional atmosphere. Focus follows subject maintaining sharp clarity, with subtle depth-of-field shifts in the background. This hold moment serves as emphasis before the cut, setting up emotional preparation for the next shot transition. Professional cinema camera operation with film-grade image stabilization and fluid pacing rhythm.
