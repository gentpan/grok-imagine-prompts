## 中文

**But that got me thinking,**

但这让我想到,一个imagine禁用词列表会非常有帮助,可以避免浪费你每天的12个视频。我不会再使用Imagine了,所以这只是一个想法,也许社区中有人想要解决这个问题来帮助其他人。显然xai(人类历史上最神秘的组织)不会发布禁用词列表,所以我们只能靠自己,就像所有与xai相关的事情一样。

## English

**But that got me thinking,**

But that got me thinking, a list of words that imagine has banned would be really helpful for not wasting your 12 videos per day. I won't be using Imagine again, so it's just an idea that maybe somebody in the community would want to tackle to help out everyone else. Obviously xai (the most secretive organization in human history) won't be putting out a list of banned words, so we're on our own, as with all things xai related.
