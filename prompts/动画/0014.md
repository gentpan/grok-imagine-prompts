## 中文

**巨型警察追逐**

一位身材巨大的超现实警察正在追逐你的第一人称视角动画。镜头采用手持运动风格，模拟奔跑时的剧烈晃动和喘息感。回望镜头显示，身后一个比建筑物还高的巨型警察迈着沉重的步伐紧追不舍，每一步都让地面震颤。都市街道场景，霓虹灯光在夜色中闪烁，营造出科幻反乌托邦氛围。镜头快速穿梭于街道、小巷之间，速度感强烈。警察的轮廓在逆光中显得更加威严庞大，警笛声和脚步声的音效暗示。色调偏冷，蓝紫色调主导，增强紧张追逐的戏剧性。广角镜头夸大空间透视，强化巨型警察的压迫感。

## English

**Colossal Policeman Chase**

A first-person perspective animation of a gigantic surreal policeman relentlessly pursuing you. Camera employs handheld motion style, simulating intense shaking and breathless sensation during running. Looking-back shots reveal an enormous policeman towering above buildings, taking heavy steps in pursuit, each footfall causing ground tremors. Urban street scene with neon lights flickering in the night, creating a dystopian sci-fi atmosphere. Camera rapidly weaving through streets and alleyways with intense sense of speed and urgency. Policeman's silhouette appears even more imposing and colossal against backlight, with implied siren sounds and thunderous footsteps. Cool color palette dominated by blue-purple tones enhancing dramatic chase tension. Wide-angle lens exaggerating spatial perspective, amplifying the oppressive presence of the giant policeman with cinematic intensity.
