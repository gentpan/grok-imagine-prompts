## 中文

**珊瑚礁之舞**

一段令人惊叹的海底珊瑚礁动画场景。镜头缓慢推进，穿过五彩斑斓的珊瑚群，各种形态的珊瑚随着洋流轻轻摇曳，如同优雅的舞蹈。阳光从水面透射下来，形成动态的光束效果，在珊瑚和沙地上投下波光粼粼的影子。色彩绚丽的热带鱼群在珊瑚间穿梭游动，增添生命活力。海葵触须柔软地飘动，海藻随波摆动。水下光线呈现出深邃的蓝绿色调，由明到暗自然过渡。粒子效果模拟浮游生物和气泡缓缓上升。镜头运动流畅平稳，营造出宁静而梦幻的海底世界氛围，3D动画质感，高清细腻。

## English

**Reef Dance**

A stunning underwater coral reef animation scene with mesmerizing beauty. Camera slowly tracks forward, gliding through vibrant colorful coral formations, various coral shapes gently swaying with ocean currents in an elegant dance-like motion. Sunlight penetrates from the water surface, creating dynamic god-ray light beam effects, casting shimmering caustic patterns on corals and sandy bottom. Brilliantly colored tropical fish schools weaving through coral structures, adding vitality and life. Sea anemones with soft tentacles flowing gracefully, seaweed swaying rhythmically with waves. Underwater lighting presents deep blue-green color palette with natural gradation from light to dark. Particle effects simulating plankton and bubbles slowly ascending. Smooth fluid camera movement creating tranquil yet dreamlike underwater world atmosphere. 3D animation quality with high-definition detail and cinematic rendering.
