## 中文

**xAI rolled out Grok Imagine**

xAI推出了Grok Imagine 1.5预览版，这是该公司最新的图生视频更新，带来了升级的真实感、音频同步和提示词跟随功能。新版本在视觉保真度上实现了显著提升，材质纹理渲染更加细腻真实，光照计算更准确，物理运动更符合自然规律。音频同步技术能够根据视觉内容智能生成匹配的环境音效、动作音效和背景音乐，实现视听一体化体验。增强的提示词跟随能力确保生成内容更精确地契合用户描述，包括复杂的镜头运动、多重动作序列和细微的氛围控制。支持更长的视频时长和更高的输出分辨率，色彩还原准确，动态范围广阔。

## English

**xAI rolled out Grok Imagine**

xAI rolled out Grok Imagine 1.5 Preview, the company's latest image-to-video update, which brings upgraded realism, audio syncing, and prompt following. New version achieves significant improvements in visual fidelity, with material texture rendering more delicate and realistic, lighting calculations more accurate, and physical motion more aligned with natural laws. Audio synchronization technology intelligently generates matching ambient sounds, action effects, and background music based on visual content, achieving integrated audiovisual experience. Enhanced prompt-following capability ensures generated content more precisely matches user descriptions, including complex camera movements, multiple action sequences, and subtle atmosphere control. Supports longer video duration and higher output resolution, accurate color reproduction, wide dynamic range.
