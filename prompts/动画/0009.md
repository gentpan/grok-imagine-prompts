## 中文

**Rooftop Reveal**

黄金时刻城市屋顶,英雄走进光中;缓慢推轨至腰部特写;最后一秒微妙地向上倾斜;柔和的轮廓光,浅景深。(摄像机控制模板)。场景设定在日落时分的城市摩天大楼屋顶，温暖的金色阳光从低角度照射，在英雄身后形成美丽的逆光光晕和柔和的轮廓光。背景是渐变的橙粉色天空和模糊的城市天际线。摄像机从全景缓慢推轨靠近，流畅地过渡到腰部以上的中近景特写，浅景深使背景融化成梦幻散景。英雄坚定地踏步向前走进光芒之中，身影从剪影逐渐显现细节。最后一秒镜头微妙地向上倾斜，展现英雄仰望天空的英勇姿态，强化史诗感和希望感。色温温暖，氛围宁静而充满力量。

## English

**Rooftop Reveal**

Golden hour city rooftop, hero steps into light; slow dolly-in to waist-up; subtle tilt up at the last second; soft rim light, shallow depth of field. (Camera controls template). Scene set on metropolitan skyscraper rooftop at sunset, warm golden sunlight illuminating from low angle, forming beautiful backlight halo and soft rim light behind hero. Background features gradient orange-pink sky and blurred city skyline. Camera slowly dollies in from wide shot, smoothly transitioning to medium close-up waist-up framing, shallow depth of field melting background into dreamy bokeh. Hero confidently strides forward into the light, silhouette gradually revealing details. Final second camera subtly tilts upward, showcasing hero's heroic posture gazing at sky, reinforcing epic and hopeful sentiment. Color temperature warm, atmosphere tranquil yet powerful.
