## 中文

**生物伊甸园 - 揭示预告片**

一个充满奇幻色彩的生物科技世界预告片场景。镜头从黑暗中缓缓推进，逐渐揭示一个由生物发光植物和未来科技融合的神秘伊甸园。巨大的生物树木散发着蓝绿色的荧光，树干上流动着能量脉冲般的光纹。空中漂浮着发光的孢子和粒子，营造出梦幻般的氛围。地面覆盖着会随触碰改变颜色的生物地毯，远处是高耸的生物建筑结构。镜头运动采用史诗级电影预告片风格，配合戏剧性的光影变化。色调以青色、紫色和绿色为主，营造出神秘而充满希望的未来感。大气透视效果明显，景深层次丰富，整体画面充满奇幻科幻氛围。

## English

**BioEden – Reveal Trailer**

A fantastical bio-tech world reveal trailer scene filled with wonder. Camera slowly pushes forward from darkness, gradually unveiling a mysterious Eden where bioluminescent flora and futuristic technology merge seamlessly. Massive bio-organic trees emanating blue-green luminescence, with energy pulse patterns flowing across trunks like living circuits. Glowing spores and particles floating through the air, creating a dreamlike atmosphere. Ground covered with bio-carpet that changes color upon touch, distant towering bio-architectural structures rising majestically. Camera movement follows epic cinematic trailer style with dramatic lighting transitions and reveals. Color palette dominated by cyan, purple, and green tones, creating mysterious yet hopeful futuristic ambiance. Strong atmospheric perspective effect with rich depth-of-field layers, overall composition filled with fantasy sci-fi atmosphere and awe-inspiring visual grandeur.
