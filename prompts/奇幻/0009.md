**I’ve seen several comments under**

I’ve been searching far and wide for any references to a policy change that would back that up and haven’t seen anything. When I asked Grok itself, it couldn’t find anything about it either. No browser change, fresh login, Venice version update, just one prompt to the next in the same chat window less than 20 seconds apart. I’m obviously hoping like the rest of y’all that it’s a temporary glitch like it’s done in the past, but if anyone has something concrete to show they updated policy I’d love to see it. ✌️ submitted by /u/contortedbrain to r/VeniceAI [link] [comments]
