## 中文

**Grok Imagine moderated the image**

Grok Imagine因潜在的版权侵犯而审核了图像,尽管我的图像是100%由我自己创建的。当然,我的图像是一个草率快速完成的风景,但它100%是我的,它拒绝了。

## English

**Grok Imagine moderated the image**

Grok Imagine moderated the image for potential copyright violation, even though my image was 100% created by myself. Sure my image was a shitty quickly done landscape, but it was 100% mine, and it refused.
