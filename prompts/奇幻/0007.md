## 中文

**被遗忘的魔法图书馆**

一座隐藏在古老森林深处的圆形图书馆，书架环绕上升至看不见顶的高度。无数古籍悬浮在空中，缓缓旋转，书页自动翻动，散发出柔和的金色光芒。图书馆中央站着一位年轻的魔法学徒，身穿深蓝色长袍，伸手触碰一本漂浮的古书。当她的手指接触书本瞬间，周围的书籍突然加速旋转，形成一道发光的螺旋。魔法粒子从书页中飘出，在空中勾勒出古老的符文。镜头采用环绕 crane shot，从学徒身后开始，缓慢上升并旋转，展现整个图书馆的宏伟结构。奇幻风格照明，暖色调为主，景深效果突出悬浮书籍的层次感。

## English

**The Forgotten Magical Library**

A circular library hidden deep in an ancient forest, with bookshelves spiraling upward to an unseen height. Countless ancient tomes float in the air, slowly rotating, pages turning automatically, emanating a soft golden glow. At the library's center stands a young magic apprentice in a deep blue robe, reaching out to touch a floating ancient book. The moment her fingers make contact, the surrounding books suddenly accelerate their rotation, forming a glowing spiral. Magical particles drift from the pages, sketching ancient runes in the air. Camera uses a circling crane shot, starting from behind the apprentice, slowly rising and rotating to reveal the library's grand structure. Fantasy-style lighting, warm tones predominant, depth of field effect highlighting the layered floating books.
