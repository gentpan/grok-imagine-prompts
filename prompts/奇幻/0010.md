## 中文

**On the static product shot**

在静态产品镜头上——瓶子固定,摄像机环绕——Seedance和Omni Flash都清晰正确地渲染了标语Unleash Your Best Self。Kling把它搞乱了。画面采用360度环绕轨道镜头拍摄,打造出流畅的产品展示动态。照明使用专业的环形光源配合顶光,确保瓶身每个角度都均匀受光,玻璃材质的折射与反射效果真实细腻。背景采用纯净的渐变色,从深色过渡到浅色,突出产品主体。标语文字清晰锐利,具有精致的印刷质感,整体呈现出高端奇幻风格产品视频的专业品质。

## English

**On the static product shot**

On the static product shot — bottle fixed, camera orbiting — Seedance and Omni Flash both rendered the tagline Unleash Your Best Self cleanly and correctly. Kling garbled it. Image captured with 360-degree orbital tracking shot creating fluid product showcase dynamics. Lighting employs professional ring light paired with top lighting ensuring even illumination from every angle, glass material refraction and reflection effects realistic and refined. Background features clean gradient transitioning from dark to light tones, emphasizing product subject. Tagline typography sharp and clear with exquisite print texture, overall presenting high-end fantasy-style product video professional quality.
