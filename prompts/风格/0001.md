## 中文

**Retro B&W**

我今天很复古——你能把这个做成旧的黑白快照吗？就像我奶奶会装裱的那种。高对比度的银盐胶片质感,带有细腻的颗粒感和轻微的暗角效果。照明呈现出经典的40-50年代摄影风格,柔和的自然光线透过窗户形成戏剧性的明暗对比。画面边缘略微泛黄褪色,带有时光流逝的痕迹。整体氛围充满怀旧情怀和温暖记忆,仿佛从家族相册中精心保存的珍贵瞬间,散发出永恒而动人的复古美感。

## English

**Retro B&W**

I'm feeling all retro today—can you make this look like an old black-and-white snapshot? Like something my grandma would've framed. High-contrast silver halide film texture with fine grain and subtle vignetting effects. Lighting showcases classic 1940s-50s photography style, with soft natural light streaming through windows creating dramatic chiaroscuro. Edges of the frame slightly yellowed and faded, bearing traces of time's passage. The overall atmosphere is filled with nostalgia and warm memories, as if a precious moment carefully preserved from a family album, radiating timeless and touching vintage beauty.
