## 中文

**Goofy Cartoon**

这太严肃了——你能把它变成带粗线条和狂野色彩的滑稽卡通吗？让我们玩得开心点！明亮饱和的照明营造出欢快活泼的氛围,高对比度的原色调色板充满能量。夸张的透视和动态构图强调喜剧效果,粗黑的轮廓线勾勒出简化的形状。扁平化的色块和鲜明的阴影呈现出经典卡通质感,没有细腻渐变。表面材质简洁明快,带有光泽反光和弹性质感。整体氛围轻松幽默,充满童趣和想象力,仿佛周六早晨的经典动画片,散发出无拘无束的快乐能量。

## English

**Goofy Cartoon**

This is too serious—can you turn it into a goofy cartoon with thick lines and wild colors? Let's have some fun with it! Bright saturated lighting creates a cheerful and lively atmosphere, with a high-contrast primary color palette full of energy. Exaggerated perspective and dynamic composition emphasize comedic effect, with thick black outlines defining simplified shapes. Flat color blocks and bold shadows present classic cartoon texture, without subtle gradients. Surface materials are clean and crisp, with glossy reflections and bouncy textures. The overall atmosphere is relaxed and humorous, full of childlike wonder and imagination, like a classic Saturday morning cartoon, radiating carefree joyful energy.
