## 中文

**Cyber Neon**

我迷上了科幻氛围——你能把这个做成到处都是霓虹灯的发光赛博朋克城市吗？让它酷到爆。强烈的霓虹灯光照明呈现出鲜艳的品红、青色和紫色,色温在6500K-8000K之间。低角度广角镜头仰拍高耸的建筑物,营造出压迫感和史诗感。湿润的街道反射着霓虹灯的炫目光芒,全息广告牌闪烁不停。金属和玻璃表面呈现出光滑反光的质感,空气中漂浮着蒸汽和光雾。氛围充满未来感和神秘感,带有黑暗都市的赛博朋克美学,散发出前卫酷炫的科幻魅力。

## English

**Cyber Neon**

I'm hooked on sci-fi vibes—can you make this a glowing cyberpunk city with neon signs everywhere? Make it crazy cool. Intense neon lighting presents vibrant magenta, cyan, and purple hues, with color temperature between 6500K-8000K. Low-angle wide lens shoots upward at towering buildings, creating a sense of oppression and epicness. Wet streets reflect dazzling neon glows, holographic billboards flicker constantly. Metal and glass surfaces show smooth reflective textures, with steam and light fog floating in the air. The atmosphere is futuristic and mysterious, with dark urban cyberpunk aesthetics, radiating cutting-edge cool sci-fi charm.
