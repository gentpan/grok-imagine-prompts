## 中文

**Watercolor Dream**

这对我来说太清晰了——你能把它模糊成柔和流动的水彩画吗？我渴望那种梦幻、艺术的感觉。

## English

**Watercolor Dream**

This is too crisp for me—can you blur it into a soft, flowy watercolor? I’m craving that dreamy, artsy feel.
