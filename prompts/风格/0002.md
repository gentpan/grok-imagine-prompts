## 中文

**Watercolor Dream**

这对我来说太清晰了——你能把它模糊成柔和流动的水彩画吗？我渴望那种梦幻、艺术的感觉。柔和漫射的照明营造出轻盈透明的氛围,色彩在湿润的纸张上自然晕开。水彩颜料的流动质感清晰可见,边缘柔和模糊,呈现出渐变和渗透效果。画面采用虚焦和浅景深,强调空灵飘逸的艺术感。色调明亮而柔和,带有水润透明的材质特性。整体氛围梦幻浪漫,充满诗意想象,仿佛清晨薄雾中的水彩诗篇,散发出宁静而优雅的艺术气息。

## English

**Watercolor Dream**

This is too crisp for me—can you blur it into a soft, flowy watercolor? I'm craving that dreamy, artsy feel. Soft diffused lighting creates a light and transparent atmosphere, with colors naturally bleeding on wet paper. The flowing texture of watercolor pigments is clearly visible, edges soft and blurred, showing gradient and seeping effects. The composition uses soft focus and shallow depth of field, emphasizing an ethereal and graceful artistic quality. Tones are bright and gentle, with water-transparent material characteristics. The overall atmosphere is dreamy and romantic, full of poetic imagination, like a watercolor poem in morning mist, radiating tranquil and elegant artistic essence.
