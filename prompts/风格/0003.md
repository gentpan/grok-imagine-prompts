## 中文

**Rainy Chill**

我现在心情很放松——能否将这个场景变成一个雨天,有黑云和湿漉漉的街道？完美适合蜷缩起来喝茶。柔和的漫射照明透过厚重的云层,呈现5000K左右的冷色调光线。雨滴在镜头上形成轻微的景深模糊,街道表面反射着天空和灯光的倒影。湿润的沥青路面展现出光滑的质感,水坑中涟漪荡漾。空气中弥漫着宁静舒适的氛围,带有雨后清新的气息。广角镜头捕捉街道的延伸感,营造出适合静思冥想的慵懒雨天场景,散发出温暖惬意的居家情怀。

## English

**Rainy Chill**

I'm in a chill mood—can you turn this into a rainy day with dark clouds and wet streets? Perfect for curling up with tea. Soft diffused lighting filters through heavy clouds, presenting cool-toned light around 5000K. Raindrops on the lens create subtle depth of field blur, while the street surface reflects the sky and light inversions. Wet asphalt pavement shows smooth texture, with ripples dancing in puddles. The atmosphere is tranquil and comfortable, with fresh post-rain air. Wide-angle lens captures the street's sense of extension, creating a lazy rainy day scene perfect for quiet contemplation, radiating warm and cozy homey sentiment.
