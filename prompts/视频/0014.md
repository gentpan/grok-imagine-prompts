## 中文

**Reverse-flow water sphere**

桌上放置一杯水，水突然向上流出杯子并形成一个漂浮的球体，照片级真实，柔和的日光。温暖的5500K自然光从侧窗照射,在木质桌面上投下柔和阴影。慢动作摄影捕捉水流的优雅运动,水珠在空中形成完美球形,内部折射出彩虹般的光芒。玻璃杯表面呈现晶莹剔透的质感,细微的水波涟漪清晰可见。背景采用浅景深虚化,营造出宁静超现实的氛围,空气中漂浮着微小的水雾粒子,整体场景充满魔幻和科学奇观的视觉张力。

## English

**Reverse-flow water sphere**

A glass of water on a table, the water suddenly flows upward out of the glass and forms a floating sphere, photorealistic, soft daylight. Warm 5500K natural light streams from a side window, casting soft shadows on the wooden table surface. Slow-motion cinematography captures the elegant movement of flowing water, droplets forming a perfect sphere in mid-air, refracting rainbow-like rays internally. The glass surface shows crystal-clear texture, with subtle water ripples clearly visible. Background uses shallow depth of field bokeh, creating a serene and surreal atmosphere, with tiny water mist particles floating in the air, the entire scene filled with the visual tension of magical and scientific wonder.
