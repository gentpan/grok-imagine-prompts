## 中文

**原子朋克赛博风格重制**

将现有视频片段的视觉风格转换为未来主义赛博朋克美学。增强霓虹色彩，主要使用电蓝色、品红色、紫色和青色的荧光配色方案。添加扫描线、像素化干扰、全息投影效果和数字故障特效。强化科技感元素，如悬浮的数字界面、LED灯带、投影广告牌和发光电路纹理。提升对比度和饱和度，营造出高科技低生活的赛博朋克氛围。城市场景增加雾气和光晕效果，制造潮湿反光的路面质感。色调校正为冷色调主导，局部加入暖色霓虹光源作为点缀。整体视觉呈现80年代复古未来主义与现代科技感的融合。

## English

**Atomic Cyberpunk Restyle**

Transform existing video clip's visual style into futuristic cyberpunk aesthetic with comprehensive stylistic overhaul. Enhance neon color palette, primarily utilizing electric blue, magenta, purple, and cyan fluorescent color scheme. Add scan lines, pixelated interference, holographic projection effects, and digital glitch artifacts. Intensify tech-aesthetic elements including floating digital interfaces, LED light strips, projection billboards, and glowing circuit patterns. Boost contrast and saturation to create high-tech low-life cyberpunk atmosphere. Urban scenes enriched with atmospheric fog and lens flare effects, producing wet reflective pavement texture. Color grading shifted toward cool-tone dominance with localized warm neon light sources as accents. Overall visual presentation merges 1980s retro-futurism with contemporary technological aesthetics in cinematic style.
