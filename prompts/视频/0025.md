## 中文

**世界杯球迷镜头**

从故事板图像生成视频。生成一个连续自然的10秒视频，而不是幻灯片。

场景：
2030年国际足联世界杯小组赛，中国对巴西，第60分钟，比分1-1。超真实的现场体育电视直播球迷镜头。保留参考图像中主要成年球迷的身份、发型、服装颜色、脸贴、体育场观众氛围、广播色彩和记分牌样式。

风格：
照片级真实，超真实直播体育广播，专业电视摄影机素材，真实的体育场光线，自然的观众运动，浅景深效果，真实的球迷镜头时刻，真实的皮肤质感，自然的面部表情，真实的广播色彩分级。

固定覆盖层：
在整个过程中在右上角保持真实的记分牌：
FIFA WORLD CUP 2030 - GROUP STAGE
CHN 1 - 1 BRA
时钟从60:18自然前进到60:28。比分保持1-1。

动作：
0-1秒：横跨拥挤的世界杯看台的广角定场镜头。
1-2秒：相机推进穿过看台，找到特写球迷。
2-3秒：自然的广播切换到主要球迷，专注于比赛。
3-4秒：她注意到相机并开始微笑。
4-5秒：温暖自然的微笑，中近景。
5-6秒：她轻轻挥手。
6-7秒：她飞吻。
7-8秒：飞吻流畅地继续。
8-10秒：她做出双手比心手势并轻轻点头。

运动顺序：
观看比赛 -> 注意到相机 -> 微笑 -> 挥手 -> 飞吻 -> 双手比心。

负面提示：
无动画，无卡通，无AI感，无塑料皮肤，无面部变化，无未成年外观，无性化，无多余手指，无畸形的手，无错误比分，无乱码文本，无缺失记分牌，无突然的场景变化。

## English

**World Cup Fan Cam**

Reference-to-video from a storyboard image. Generate one continuous natural 10-second video, not a slideshow.

Scene:
2030 FIFA World Cup group stage, China vs Brazil, 60th minute, score 1-1. Ultra-realistic live sports TV broadcast fan-cam. Preserve the reference image's main adult fan identity, hairstyle, outfit colors, face sticker, stadium crowd atmosphere, broadcast colors, and scoreboard style.

Style:
photorealistic, ultra realistic live sports broadcast, professional TV camera footage, realistic stadium lighting, natural crowd movement, shallow depth of field, authentic fan cam moment, realistic skin texture, natural facial expressions, real broadcast color grading.

Fixed overlay:
Keep a realistic scoreboard in the top right throughout:
FIFA WORLD CUP 2030 - GROUP STAGE
CHN 1 - 1 BRA
The clock advances naturally from 60:18 to 60:28. Score stays 1-1.

Action:
0-1s wide establishing shot across packed World Cup stands.
1-2s camera pushes in through the stands and finds the featured fan.
2-3s natural broadcast cut to the main fan, focused on the match.
3-4s she notices the camera and begins smiling.
4-5s warm natural smile, medium close-up.
5-6s she gently waves.
6-7s she gives a flying kiss.
7-8s flying kiss continues smoothly.
8-10s she makes a two-hand heart gesture and nods softly.

Motion order:
watching match -> noticing camera -> smiling -> waving -> flying kiss -> two-hand heart.

Negative:
No animation, no cartoon, no AI look, no plastic skin, no face change, no underage look, no sexualization, no extra fingers, no deformed hands, no wrong score, no garbled text, no missing scoreboard, no sudden scene change.
